# Combat Basics

Core systems that every Time Patroller should understand.

## Stamina
- Used for vanishing, certain dashes, blocking heavy attacks, and some skills.
- Depleting stamina leads to **Guard Break** (vulnerable state until stamina recovers).
- Manage carefully — over-vanishing is a common beginner mistake.

## Vanish (Z-Vanish)
- Offensive Vanish (1 stamina bar): Teleport behind/near opponent, useful for combo extensions or repositioning.
- Defensive Vanish (2 stamina bars): Escape hitstun / combos when locked on and close enough.
- Opponents can often “back-hit” predictable vanishes. Mix with steps, blocks, and evasive skills.

## Stamina Break
- Specific inputs (directional + attack) or certain skills can break an opponent’s stamina, leaving them open.
- High-value in both PvE and PvP for creating punish windows.

## Other Fundamentals
- Ki management for supers and ultimates (charge skills help).
- Just Guard / perfect blocking timing reduces damage and can create openings.
- Evasive Skills provide invincibility frames or repositioning at a stamina or cooldown cost.
- Transformation (Awoken Skills) dramatically changes damage, mobility, and available options.
