# Beginner & Priority Guides

## Quick-Start Checklist (New Player Priority)
1. Complete the main story at least through the Cell Saga (unlocks Expert Missions).
2. Unlock flight (Frieza Saga) → talk to **Nit** for the **40 Ton Weights** Super Soul.
3. Visit the five Time Rifts and begin collecting Distorted Time Eggs.
4. Start mentor training (Krillin early, then others as they appear).
5. Complete Advancement Tests → aim for Z-Ranks to unlock Potential Unleashed.
6. Raise attribute caps via Guru and later Whis foods.
7. Craft early QQ Bangs at Capsule Corp.
8. Farm Parallel Quests for Dragon Balls and skills; use Shenron for key unlocks and medals.
9. Once Expert Missions are available, farm EM 16/17 for TP Medals and use Tosok for efficient leveling.
10. Progress Future Saga Extra Missions when ready for Beast / Power to Overcome and endgame content.

## Recommended Early Goals
- Potential Unleashed (universal strong Awoken Skill).
- A solid QQ Bang and at least one good farming Super Soul.
- Access to Conton City shops and Transfer robots.
- At least one reliable Expert Mission clear for TP income.

## Mid-to-Late Game
- Level 120–199 via TP Medal + Tosok or heavy XP farming with score Super Souls.
- Collect remaining Distorted Time Eggs → Unknown History.
- Partner Customization Keys via Raids.
- Crystal Raids for extra skills and clothing.
- Future Saga full completion for the final Awoken Skill and story.
