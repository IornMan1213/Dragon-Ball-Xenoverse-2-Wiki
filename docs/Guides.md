# Beginner & Priority Guides

## Quick-Start Checklist (New Player Priority)
1. Complete the main story far enough to unlock the major Conton City systems and Expert Missions.
2. Unlock flight and start building a reliable early farming setup.
3. Visit the five Time Rifts and work toward the Distorted Time Eggs if you want the extra story.
4. Start mentor training as instructors become available.
5. Complete Advancement Tests and work toward Potential Unleashed.
6. Raise attribute caps through the Guru progression and later endgame systems.
7. Craft a useful QQ Bang at Capsule Corp rather than chasing a perfect roll immediately.
8. Farm Parallel Quests for skills, Dragon Balls and equipment; inspect the reward list before committing to a long grind.
9. Once Expert Missions are available, establish a repeatable TP Medal/XP route.
10. Progress Future Saga content when your character is ready for late-game rewards and newer Awoken Skills.

## Recommended Early Goals
- **Potential Unleashed** as a broadly useful universal Awoken Skill.
- A serviceable QQ Bang with positives in your preferred damage stat.
- Reliable Ki and Stamina management rather than immediately maxing every attribute.
- At least one safe farming Super Soul and one fast-clearing Super Attack.
- A small collection of mentors so your skill pool grows naturally while progressing.

## How to Choose What to Farm
### Need a specific skill?
Start with the skill's database record. Check **skill type → source → PQ/mentor/TP Shop/raid → DLC requirement → Ultimate Finish condition** before farming.

### Need TP Medals?
Compare Expert Missions, raids and other repeatable activities in the [Farming Hub](Farming-Hub.md). The best route changes with your build, available DLC and whether you are farming solo or online.

### Need levels?
Use Tosok and XP sources when available, but do not burn TP Medals on levels if the same currency is needed for the skill or shop item you actually want.

## Mid-to-Late Game
- Level toward the current cap while keeping a build goal in mind.
- Collect remaining Distorted Time Eggs → Unknown History.
- Collect Partner Customization Keys from the relevant Raid events.
- Use Crystal Raids when chasing their exclusive reward pools.
- Complete Future Saga chapters for their story, skills, Super Souls and late-game systems.
- Build separate PvE and PvP loadouts rather than forcing one setup to do everything.

## Database-First Workflow

**Search → verify source → check requirements → farm → record result → optimize build.**
