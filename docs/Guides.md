# Guides & Priority Paths

## Recommended New Player Route
1. Finish the main story (unlocks flying after Frieza Saga, more mentors, Conton City features).
2. Start Advancement Tests immediately (Easy → God Class) for Potential Unleashed and broader mentor access.
3. Train early mentors (Krillin line → Piccolo / Tien / Yamcha) for core skills.
4. Complete race-specific Time Rift questlines for racial Awoken Skills.
5. Raise Friendship with key mentors (especially Piccolo + Gohan/Videl for Beast).
6. Farm TP Medals (Expert Mission 16 and high-value PQs) and Dragon Balls.
7. Raise level cap via Guru then Whis food items.
8. Work through DLC stories, saving Future Saga Chapter 4 for last (unlocks The Power to Overcome).

## High-Value Farming
- **TP Medals / STP**: Expert Mission 16 (and later variants), Crystal-related events when available, high-score Parallel Quests.
- **Dragon Balls**: Rotate PQs that spawn Time Patrollers. Avoid immediately repeating the exact same PQ.
- **Zeni**: Story, PQs, Shenron “I want money!”.
- **Experience**: South Supreme Kai Super Soul (obtained from Nit after story) + weights + score-boosting Super Souls.

## Expert Missions
Many can be soloed offline with Death Ball spam + suitable Awoken (Potential Unleashed / Golden / etc.) + Ki Blast-focused QQ Bang + appropriate Super Souls (classic community strategy). Later missions and raids benefit from co-op.

## Other Systems
- Conton City is large — use map markers (pink for mentors).
- Distorted Time Eggs (one per major rift) lead to special rewards from the Supreme Kai of Time.
- Partner Customization Keys drop from Raids and unlock extra characters for Partner Customization.
