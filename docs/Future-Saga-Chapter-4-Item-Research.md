## Evidence policy

- Official/platform listings establish the Chapter 4 content count but not every individual item name or acquisition condition.
- The July 2026 PQ guide is secondary evidence and is labeled accordingly.
- Community reports are retained as research leads rather than silently upgraded to verified facts.
- No drop rates, prices, damage values, or unsupported normal/Ultimate Finish mappings are inferred.
