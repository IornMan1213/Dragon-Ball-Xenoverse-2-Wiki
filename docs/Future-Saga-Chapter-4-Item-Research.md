A July 2026 guide identifies **The Power to Overcome** as the Chapter 4 Awoken Skill and reports that it is obtained by completing the final Chapter 4 story mission. This is treated as secondary evidence until reconciled with an official in-game/item source. The official DLC listing independently confirms that Chapter 4 contains four new moves including one Awoken Skill, but does not name it or provide its individual unlock route.

## Evidence policy

- Official/platform listings establish the Chapter 4 content count but not individual item names or every unlock condition.
- The July 2026 guide used for The Power to Overcome is secondary evidence and is labeled accordingly.
- Community reports are retained as research leads rather than silently upgraded to verified facts.
- No drop rates, prices, damage values, or exact reward tables are inferred.
