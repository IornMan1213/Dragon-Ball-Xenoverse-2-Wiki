## EM16 — In the Realm of the Gods: Vegeta

### Identity

| Verification | `partially_verified` |

### Objective and timer

The documented objective is to defeat SSGSS Vegeta before the mission timer expires. Older independent documentation gives a **five-minute** timer for EM16 and describes a health-regeneration behavior that can restore Vegeta when pressure is reduced. The wiki treats those details as research evidence rather than final universal values until current behavior is reconciled.

### Boss behavior to document

- SSGSS Vegeta's normal combat moveset and evasive behavior.
- Health regeneration trigger, amount, timing, and interruption conditions.
- Any differences between offline and multiplayer boss behavior.
- Whether timer, boss health, or other parameters change with player count.
- Exact conditions for mission completion and failure.

### Rewards

The current indexed reward layer records **30,000 Zeni and 16 TP Medals**, with **Super Spirit Bomb** listed as the skill reward. The 16-medal value is corroborated by multiple current and historical references. The skill reward still requires mission-specific acquisition verification before the record is promoted.

Do not interpret the 30,000-Zeni figure as a statement about every possible Zeni modifier, event multiplier, or Super Soul effect.

### Farming notes

Community reports frequently use EM16 for repeat TP Medal farming because clears can be short when optimized. This is a strategy observation, not a canonical ranking. Historical player reports also disagree on whether EM16 or EM17 is faster for a given build or mode.

### Unknowns / verification queue

- Exact current timer and all timer modifiers.
- Exact regeneration formula and trigger behavior.
- Exact EXP and Zeni variation by rank/mode/modifiers.
- Exact skill-drop conditions and whether any first-clear distinction exists.
- Complete boss attack/phase table.
- Exact offline-vs-online numerical differences.

### Boss behavior to document

- Starting timer.
- Damage thresholds that extend the timer.

- Health behavior and differences from EM16.

Current references consistently identify **16 TP Medals** for EM17. The discrepancy is deliberately preserved until the actual mission reward table is reconciled.

### Farming notes

Player reports commonly describe EM17 as a repeatable TP Medal farming mission, but reported clear times depend heavily on build, platform, player count, and whether the run is offline. These reports must not be converted into a universal best-method claim.

## Shared Reward and Economy Notes

### TP Medals

Current references document 16 TP Medals for EM16/17, 18 for EM18/19, and 20 for EM20. Historical player reports independently corroborate the 16-medal EM16/17 and 18-medal EM18/19 figures. These are reward facts, not a recommendation about which mission a player should choose.

### Zeni and EXP

The indexed Zeni values above come from the current mission reward reference. Actual received currency/experience can be affected by mission conditions or external modifiers; those modifiers must be documented separately rather than baked into the canonical reward field.

### Demon Realm Crystals

Expert Missions participate in the Demon Realm Crystal economy. This page intentionally does not invent a per-mission probability or quantity. A later data layer should distinguish guaranteed rewards, observed drops, event modifiers, and community-reported rates.

### Skill drops

A skill appearing in a mission's reward list does not by itself establish a drop percentage. The wiki will record the skill association now and add probability/condition fields only when independently supported.
