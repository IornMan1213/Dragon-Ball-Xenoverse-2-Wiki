# Level Cap & Attributes

## Attribute Points
There are six attributes:
- Health
- Max Ki
- Max Stamina
- Basic Attacks
- Strike Supers
- Ki Blast Supers

Points are gained by leveling up. The total available points increases with higher level caps. At high levels (approaching 199) players have a large pool to distribute.

### Key Thresholds
- Ki and Stamina bars scale with points invested + QQ Bang bonuses.
- With a strong (+5) QQ Bang focused on Ki/Stamina, far fewer attribute points are needed to reach high bar counts (community data shows very efficient breakpoints at level 199).
- Maximum points per individual attribute can be raised through Guru’s potential unlock and later Whis-related progression (up to 200 in a single stat in later level caps).

### Practical Distribution Tips (Level 199 Era)
- Decide your primary damage type (Basic / Strike / Ki Blast) and invest heavily there.
- Use QQ Bang to cover Ki and Stamina bars as efficiently as possible, freeing points for Health and damage stats.
- Health investment varies by race and preferred survivability.
- Respec is available via Shenron (“I want a second chance at life!”).

Level caps have been raised multiple times across the game’s life, culminating in the high caps available after Future Saga content.

**Sources**: dbxv2.fandom.com Attributes page, community level 199 spreadsheets and guides, player-reported breakpoints for Ki/Stamina bars with QQ Bangs.
