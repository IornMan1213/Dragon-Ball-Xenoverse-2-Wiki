# Expert Missions

Expert Missions are challenging co-op (or soloable offline) encounters accessed via purple rifts / the Expert Mission interface after sufficient story progress (typically after the Cell Saga tutorials).

They reward Zeni, experience, **TP Medals**, Demon Realm Crystals (for Crystal Raids), and occasional skill drops.

## Best for TP Medal Farming
Community consensus strongly favors:
- **EM 16 – In the Realm of the Gods: Vegeta** (SSGSS Vegeta) — ~16 TP Medals, very fast clears
- **EM 17 – In the Realm of the Gods: Goku** (SSGSS Goku) — ~16 TP Medals, very fast clears
- **EM 18 – The Ultimate Evil: Broly** — Slightly higher medals (~18) + crystals, a bit harder

Later missions (19–20) offer more medals but take longer. Online clears often yield more medals than offline.

Classic solo strategy (especially older content): Death Ball / strong Ki Blast spam with a suitable Awoken Skill (Potential Unleashed, Golden, Super Vegeta, etc.), Ki Blast-focused QQ Bang, and Super Souls that boost Ki Blast damage or help with stamina break.
