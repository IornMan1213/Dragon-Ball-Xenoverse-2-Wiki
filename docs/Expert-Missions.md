# Expert Missions

Expert Missions are high-difficulty co-op (or offline with AI) encounters against Superbosses. They award Zeni, TP Medals, a chance at Demon Realm Crystals, and specific skills.

## Best for TP Medal Farming: Expert Missions 16–18

These three are the community-preferred missions for efficient TP Medal farming due to their short clear times and solid medal payouts.

### Expert Mission 16 – In the Realm of the Gods: Vegeta
- **Enemy**: SSGSS Vegeta
- **Time Limit**: Approximately 5 minutes
- **Rewards**: 30,000 Zeni + **16 TP Medals** + Super Spirit Bomb (skill)
- **Notes**: Single target. Vegeta has health regeneration. Keep up consistent damage pressure. Very popular for fast medal runs.

### Expert Mission 17 – In the Realm of the Gods: Goku
- **Enemy**: SSGSS Goku
- **Rewards**: 30,000 Zeni + **16 TP Medals** + Spirit Sword (skill)
- **Notes**: Single target. Goku has stamina-related mechanics / regeneration elements. Similar difficulty and speed to Mission 16. Excellent paired with 16 for medal farming.

### Expert Mission 18 – The Ultimate Evil: Broly
- **Enemy**: Broly (Supervillain)
- **Rewards**: 35,000 Zeni + **18 TP Medals** + Focus Flash (skill) + chance at other raid-related items
- **Notes**: Slightly higher medal payout than 16/17. Broly has super armor and powerful area attacks (including Peerless Storm / Gigantic Ki Blast style moves). Many players prefer this one for the extra 2 medals when their build can clear it quickly.

## Farming Tips for 16–18
- These missions are significantly faster and more consistent than most other Expert Missions.
- Online with a coordinated or competent group is ideal, but all three can be cleared offline with AI using strong Ki Blast spam or stamina-break strategies.
- Best during double TP Medal campaigns.
- Use score-boosting Super Souls (especially 40 Ton Weights or My Hobbies Are Reading and Sports) if also grinding experience.
- Recommended approach: High Ki Blast damage builds, charge skills, and tools to deal with super armor or large area attacks (especially on Broly).
