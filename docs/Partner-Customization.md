# Partner Customization & Customization Unlock Keys

Partner Customization allows deeper modification of certain characters (skills, colors, stats presets, Super Souls) when used as partners.

**Customization Unlock Keys** drop from Raid Quests / related content and unlock additional characters for this system. Each key corresponds to a specific character.

## Example Keys (partial list from community sources)
Keys unlock characters such as:
- Goku (Super Saiyan 4), Vegeta (Super Saiyan 4), Trunks
- SSGSS Vegito / Gogeta, Tapion, Rosé Goku Black
- Android 17 (DB Super), Janemba, Broly (Full Power Super Saiyan)
- Goku (GT), Omega Shenron, Majin Buu (Gohan Absorbed)
- Jiren, Kefla (Super Saiyan), Goku (Super Saiyan God), Gogeta (SSJ4)
- Vegeta (Super Saiyan God), Super Baby 2, Goku Black, and others added over time.

Once unlocked, further options (moves, colors, etc.) are often purchased with TP Medals inside the Partner Customization interface.
