# Sales, Reception & Longevity

## Sales
- Early shipments: over 1.4 million shortly after launch.
- Progressive milestones: 5 million (by early 2019), 6 million (2020), 7 million (late 2020), **over 10 million copies shipped worldwide by May 2023**.
- Nintendo Switch version alone surpassed 500,000 by 2018.
- Continues to generate sales through DLC packs, next-gen upgrades (2024), and frequent discounts.

## Player Base
- Producer Masayuki Hirano stated in 2023 that the game still attracted approximately **1 million players per month** worldwide seven years after release.
- Steam concurrent players typically range from several hundred to a few thousand, with significant spikes during major DLC launches and events (peaks exceeding 8,000–19,000 historically depending on the source and period).
- Steam review score remains **Very Positive** (~90% positive across tens of thousands of reviews).

## Critical Reception
Launch reviews were generally positive-to-mixed:
- Metacritic scores around the low-to-mid 70s.
- Praise for anime visuals, character roster, customization, and the expanded Conton City hub.
- Criticism focused on controls, repetition, and similarity to the first *Xenoverse*.

Long-term support and continuous content updates have been widely credited for the game’s exceptional longevity.
