# Main Story (Time Patrol) Walkthrough – Text Overview

The main story consists of a series of Time Patrol missions that take the player through altered versions of classic *Dragon Ball* eras. Progress unlocks mentors, Expert Missions, higher-level content, and Conton City systems.

## Recommended Order (Community Consensus)
Earth History (Saiyan) → Namek/Frieza → Android/Cell → Future Trunks → Buu → Battle of Gods / Resurrection F → Demon Realm climax.

## Key Story Beats (Local Summary)
1. **Prologue / Raditz** — Assist Goku and Piccolo against an empowered Raditz.
2. **Saiyan Saga** — Intervene against Turles, Great Ape forms of Nappa/Vegeta, protect key events.
3. **Namek / Frieza Saga** — Ginyu Force body-swap complications, Frieza + Cooler, Dragon Balls.
4. **Android / Cell Saga** — Altered Android threats, Cell Games interference.
5. **Future Trunks Timeline** — Protect Future Gohan, deal with Mira/Towa influence.
6. **Buu Saga** — Broly interference, Kid Buu, fusion support.
7. **Battle of Gods / Resurrection F** — Beerus and Golden Frieza related alterations.
8. **Demon Realm Climax** — Towa attempts to erase the previous Future Warrior by killing Shenron; player restores the timeline. Mira absorbs Towa and Tokitoki’s egg; final confrontation with Goku’s assistance.

After the main story, Conton City systems (Expert Missions, full mentor access, Time Rifts for Distorted Time Eggs) fully open. Future Saga DLC chapters continue the narrative with Fu and Ultra Supervillain threats.
