# Mentor Skills — Full Database

Every base-game mentor teaches exactly 4 skills through a fixed sequence: an **Initiation Test** (unlocked immediately on meeting them), then **Lesson 1 → Lesson 2 → Lesson 3**, each gated behind a higher Training/Advancement rank (roughly Intermediate → Advanced → Super, though exact rank gates vary by mentor). Lesson 3 is usually the mentor's signature **Ultimate Attack**.

**Every mentor also has a DUAL Ultimate Attack**, separate from the 4 lesson skills — unlocked once your Friendship level with that mentor is maxed out, and only usable if that mentor is actively fighting alongside you (as your instructor, or as an ally summon in some quests). This is a universal system, not specific to any one mentor.

This table covers all **25 base-game mentors** (DLC mentors — Whis, Cooler, Android 16, Future Gohan, Bardock, Hit, and everyone added in later packs — are a good next batch; see the note at the bottom).

## Detailed effects (verified, not guessed)
| Skill | Type | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Destructo-Disc / Chain Destructo-Disc (Krillin) | Ki Blast Super → Ultimate | Spinning energy disc that cuts through the target; can't be guarded against. Chain Destructo-Disc is the upgraded multi-disc Ultimate version. |
| Special Beam Cannon (Piccolo) | Ultimate | Drilling twin-finger energy beam; Piccolo's signature high-damage single-target attack. |
| Evil Explosion (Piccolo) | Ki Blast Super | Piccolo releases a burst of demonic energy outward from his body. |
| Genocide Shell (Nappa) | Strike Super | Plants a ki minefield on the ground that detonates when the opponent is near it. |
| Arm Crash (Nappa) | Strike Super | A guard-breaking rush strike — on hit, it depletes the opponent's stamina and forces Guard Break state (they temporarily can't use stamina-cost actions). |
| Kamehameha-family moves (x10/Super Kamehameha, Perfect Kamehameha, etc.) | Ki Blast Super/Ultimate | Two-handed beam attack, the Turtle School's signature technique; variants differ in width, charge time, and power multiplier. |
| Galick Gun / Final Flash (Vegeta) | Ki Blast Super / Ultimate | Vegeta's signature purple energy wave (Galick Gun) and his devastating charged energy cannon (Final Flash). |
| Death Beam / Death Ball (Frieza) | Ki Blast Super / Ultimate | A thin, high-precision finger beam (Death Beam) and Frieza's iconic giant charged sphere thrown at the target (Death Ball). |

| Instant Transmission (Goku) | Evasive | Goku's signature teleportation technique — locks onto a ki signature and closes distance instantly. |
| Spirit Bomb (Goku) | Ki Blast Super/Ultimate | Goku gathers energy from surrounding life to form a massive sphere; iconic finishing move. |

| Super Ghost Kamikaze Attack (Gotenks) | Ultimate | Creates exploding ghost clones that chase the opponent down. |

| Recoome Kick (Strike Super used by several presets) | Strike Super | Grants a damage-boost buff on use, making it a popular pick for melee-focused builds. |
| Meteor Crash (default Strike Super) | Strike Super | A rush attack that locks the opponent in place while held down, allowing very long combo strings (30+ hits reported). |

## Scope note — what's still missing
- **DLC mentors** (Whis, Cooler, Android 16, Future Gohan, Bardock, Hit, Champa, Vados, and everyone added in Super/Extra/Ultra/Legendary/Hero of Justice/Future Saga packs) aren't covered yet.
- **Dual Ultimates** are confirmed to exist for every mentor (universal system), but the specific move is only independently confirmed for about a third of the 25 base-game mentors above — the rest are marked "(not yet sourced)" rather than guessed.
- **Effect descriptions** for most of the ~90 named skills in the main table still need sourcing per-skill from Fandom's individual pages — type (Strike/Ki Blast/Evasive/Ultimate) has been added where confirmed, but the mechanical effect (damage, guardability, special properties) is still unverified for most entries.
- **Talk Skills / Super Souls** (passive buffs) aren't covered on this page at all yet.
