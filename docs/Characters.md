# Characters & Presets Unlock Overview

The vast majority of the roster and their alternate presets are unlocked through a combination of:

1. **Main Story progression** — Completing each saga unlocks core fighters and many base presets (Saiyan, Frieza, Android/Cell, Buu, Future Trunks, etc.).
2. **Parallel Quests** — Many alternate forms, costumes, and specific characters require clearing particular PQs (often with Ultimate Finish conditions). Completing all base + DLC PQs unlocks the bulk of the roster variants.
3. **Shenron Wishes** — “I want more usable characters!” progressively unlocks Hit, Eis Shenron, Nuova Shenron, and Omega Shenron.
4. **DLC / Future Saga** — Large batches of new playable characters and Ultra Supervillain forms come with each paid chapter (and some free updates).
5. **Partner Customization Keys** — Dropped from Raids; unlock additional characters for the Partner Customization system (skills, colors, stats, Super Souls).
6. **Other** — Certain secret forms (e.g. Super Saiyan 3 Bardock) tied to Distorted Time Eggs / Unknown History; Festival of Universes and event rewards for temporary or special presets.
