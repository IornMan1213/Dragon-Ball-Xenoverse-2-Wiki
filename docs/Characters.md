# Character Encyclopedia

The character section is being built as a **record-by-record encyclopedia**, not merely a roster list. The source reconciliation currently contains **149 canonical character identities** from a source category that exposed 155 items, plus a separately tracked 54-entry DLC-character category. The difference is intentional: category/template/race-navigation items are not silently counted as characters.

## What a complete character entry means

A character entry is considered complete only when the wiki can explain the character rather than merely name them. The target writeup covers:

- **Identity:** canonical name, aliases, naming variants, and the form/preset distinction.
- **Role:** playable fighter, NPC, boss, partner, story opponent, raid opponent, or other documented status.
- **Race/species:** the game's relevant classification.
- **Forms:** transformations and separately represented variants.
- **Presets:** what makes a preset different from the base character or another form.
- **Unlock:** exact route, prerequisite and content source when verified.
- **Skills:** notable equipped/associated skills and character-exclusive skills where documented.
- **Awoken state:** transformation or Awoken Skill information when applicable.
- **DLC/update provenance:** which DLC, free update, event or base-game content introduced the record.
- **Partner Customization:** eligibility and key/customization requirements when applicable.
- **Mission appearances:** story, Parallel Quest, Expert Mission, raid and other relevant appearances.
- **Rewards/relationships:** skills, Super Souls, clothing, keys and other content connected to the character.
- **Gameplay notes:** documented behavior, restrictions and useful distinctions between variants.
- **Verification:** what is sourced, what is cross-checked, and what remains unresolved.

The database contract deliberately leaves gameplay fields unresolved until they are independently sourced; the wiki does **not** invent missing mechanics just to make a record look complete.

## Search-first design

The roster is written so that ordinary search terms can lead into the right material. Searching for `Goku`, for example, should eventually surface his forms, presets, associated skills, quests, DLC, Super Souls and other pages that discuss him. Searching for `Super Saiyan`, `Ultra Instinct`, `Supervillain`, `Partner Customization`, or a specific quest number should likewise surface the pages where those concepts are actually explained.

The published site now has a blanket full-text search at **[/Search/](/Search/)**. New character prose automatically becomes part of the search corpus when the site rebuilds.

## Unlock sources

Character acquisition can come from several different systems, and those systems must not be collapsed into a generic “unlockable” label:

1. **Main Story** — core progression and story-linked roster access.
2. **Parallel Quests** — alternate fighters, forms, opponents and content associated with quest completion or Ultimate Finish conditions.
3. **Shenron Wishes** — selected unlocks and progression rewards.
4. **DLC / Future Saga / free updates** — characters and variants introduced outside the original roster.
5. **Partner Customization Keys** — selected partner customization content tied to Raid-related keys.
6. **Special/event content** — Time Rift/Unknown History, Festival and other limited or special systems.

A character being present in the roster is therefore not the same thing as documenting every form, preset, customization option or acquisition path associated with that character.

## Record relationships

The final encyclopedia should let a reader move in both directions:

- **Character → skills:** equipped, exclusive and associated skills.
- **Character → quests:** where the character appears and which rewards are connected to them.
- **Character → DLC:** the pack/update/event that introduced the record.
- **Character → forms:** base identity, transformation and variant relationships.
- **Character → presets:** exact playable configurations where documented.
- **Character → partner customization:** keys, customization options and eligible partners.
- **Skill → character:** who uses, teaches or provides the skill.
- **Quest → character:** enemies, allies, unlocks and reward relationships.

## Verification policy

- **Indexed:** the identity is established by the source index, but gameplay details are not assumed.
- **Partially verified:** some fields such as unlocks, forms or skills have been independently checked.
- **Verified:** the required factual fields have been checked against an authoritative or corroborated source set.

This distinction is important because the source category itself contains navigation/template/race-related material alongside character records; the project explicitly reconciles those into canonical identities rather than copying the source category blindly.
