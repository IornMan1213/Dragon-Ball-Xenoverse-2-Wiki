# Characters & Roster Unlocks

- Base roster unlocked progressively through story and PQs.
- Presets (different costumes/forms) often require specific story clears or PQs.
- DLC characters: Purchase the pack, then usually clear associated Extra Missions / Parallel Quests / story arcs.
- Partner Customization Keys: Drop from Raid Quests (unlocks additional characters for customization).
- Festival of Universes and Conton City Vote packs add limited-time / vote-chosen characters.

Full unlock guides (including every preset) are covered in long-form YouTube videos by creators such as Burcol (search "unlock every character Xenoverse 2" with current DLC number).

See also Fandom character category pages.
