# Characters & Roster Unlocks

- Base roster characters and many presets unlock progressively through the main story and Parallel Quests.
- Additional presets (different costumes/forms) frequently require specific story clears or PQ conditions.
- **DLC characters**: Purchase the corresponding pack, then usually clear associated Extra Missions, Parallel Quests, or story arcs.
- **Partner Customization Keys**: Drop from Raid Quests. Each key unlocks a specific character for the Partner Customization system (allows deeper customization of that character as a partner).
- Festival of Universes and Conton City Vote packs have added limited or vote-chosen characters over the years.

Full unlock paths for every character and every preset are lengthy. The most complete video references are long-form guides that go DLC-by-DLC.
