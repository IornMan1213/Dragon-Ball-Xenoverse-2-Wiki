# Skills Master Database

Dragon Ball Xenoverse 2 has an estimated **400+ unique skills** across the whole game — this is a different, much larger scope than [Skills-Detail.md](Skills-Detail.md), which only covers the ~100 skills taught specifically by the 25 base-game mentors. This page is the umbrella index for *everything*: default skills, TP Medal Shop skills, Parallel Quest rewards, Shenron wish rewards, race-locked skills, and Awoken Skills/Transformations, organized the same way the game (and the Fandom wiki) categorizes them.

**Honest scope note up front**: this page is a structural skeleton with confirmed category listings, populated with real verified effect notes where sourced — it is not yet a complete 400-entry database with a description for every skill. Treat the category lists below as reliable (skill names, which category they belong to), and the "Verified Effects" table as the reliable subset of "what does it actually do." Everything else is a name only, flagged as such rather than guessed at.

## The Category System
Every attack in the game falls into one of four **skill classes**, each split into **four subtypes**:

| Class | Subtypes | Slots per character |
|---|---|---|
| **Super Skills** (Super Attacks) | Strike Supers, Ki Blast Supers, Power Up Supers, Other Supers | 4 |
| **Ultimate Skills** (Ultimate Attacks) | Strike Ultimates, Ki Blast Ultimates, Other Ultimates (no "Power Up Ultimate" — Other covers non-damage effects like healing, body-changing, forced lock-on) | 2 |
| **Evasive Skills** | Strike Evasives, Ki Blast Evasives, Power Up Evasives, Other Evasives | 1 |
| **Awoken Skills / Transformations** | Not subdivided — one slot, includes all transformations (Super Saiyan family, Kaioken, Become Giant, Turn Golden, Potential Unleashed, Purification, Power Pole Pro, etc.) | 1 |

Strike-type skills are generally short-range physical attacks (including grabs and charge-in rush attacks); Ki Blast-types are generally longer-range energy attacks that often need a knockback/knockdown setup to land reliably; Power Up types grant a temporary buff; Other is the catch-all utility category (ki charges, stamina restoration, non-damaging movement/defense).

There are also several **race-locked skill categories** — skills only usable by CaCs of a specific race (or by cast characters of that race):
- **Saiyan Skills**
- **Namekian Skills** (4 known: Become Giant, Darkness Rush (Ranged), Evil Flight Strike, Namek Finger)
- **Majin Skills** (10 known: Buu Buu Ball, Candy Beam (Evasive), Darkness Rush (Melee), Evil Flight Strike, Explosive Buu Buu Punch, Go-Go Gum, Ill Bomber, Majin Kamehameha, Purification, Quick Sleep)
- Frieza Race and Human/Earthling also have race-tagged pools, not yet indexed here.

Plus two structural categories:
- **Counter Skills** — a tag for skills with a counter-attack property, cutting across the class system above (19 of 25 known: Absolute Zero, Burst Reflection, Burst Rush, Change The Future, Counter Burst, Counter Impact, Demon Flash Strike, Dimensional Hole, Dragon Burn, Dragon Thunder, Flash Fist Crush, God Breaker, Heroic Counter, Pressure Sign, Punisher Shield, Reverse Mabakusenko, Rough Ranger, Shadow Crusher, Sudden Death Beam, Super Afterimage, Super God Shock Flash — 6 more not yet retrieved)
- **Posing Skills** — cosmetic/taunt skills, not yet indexed here
- **Unavailable for CaC** — skills exclusive to preset cast characters, can't be equipped on a custom character

## Verified Effects (sourced from player-confirmed mechanics, not guessed)
This is the reliable core of the database — skills where an actual effect, cost, or mechanical property has been confirmed, not inferred from the name.

| Skill | Class | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Justice Combination (Gohan & Videl's Ultimate) | Strike Ultimate | No stamina break required to land; opponents generally need to evade to avoid it. Widely cited as one of the best Strike Ultimates, doing an estimated 8,000–14,000+ damage depending on Super Soul support. |
| Darkness Rush (Lord Slug) | Strike Ultimate | Cannot be blocked. Effective against several specific guard "poses," after a back-hit stagger, and in raids where uninterrupted damage matters. |
| Spirit Sword (Vegito) | Strike Ultimate | Costs 400 Ki. Stuns the opponent, then delivers two heavy damage ticks. No combo setup needed — works straight out of a vanish. |
| Instant Severance | Strike/Ki Ultimate | Effective in mid-range combos and ground-level combos; also useful against airborne opponents. |
| Giant Storm (Nappa) | Ki Blast Ultimate | Summons a large ground explosion under the opponent; only costs 3 Ki bars; widely cited as reliable, high-damage, and fast-casting. |
| Perfect Kamehameha (Cell) | Ki Blast Ultimate | Strong "pre-beam" tracking — the beam adjusts toward the target even before the initial punch/setup connects. |
| Death Ball (Frieza) | Ki Blast Ultimate | Very good tracking; costs 4 Ki bars. |
| Heat Dome Attack | Ki Blast Ultimate/Super | Good against being surrounded by multiple enemies; used at close range, leaves little opening for the opponent to guard-break the user. |
| Super Nova (Cooler) | Ki Blast Ultimate | Large area, high damage. |
| Serious Bomb | Strike/Ki Ultimate (punch + blast combo) | If the initial punch connects, the follow-up ki blast is very difficult for the opponent to escape or evade. |
| Saiyan Spirit | Strike Ultimate, Saiyan-exclusive | Can combo directly from basic attacks; good finisher. |
| Big Bang Kamehameha | Ki Blast Super, Super Saiyan-only | Teleports the user in front of the opponent when fully charged, making it easier to land; high damage. |

| Recoome Kick | Strike Super | Grants a damage-boost buff on use. |
| Maiden Blast (Pan) | Ultimate | Comparatively weak damage, but stunlocks the target and can be comboed into from almost any setup. |

## Scope note — what's still missing
- **Full category listings**: only Majin (10/10) and Namekian (4/4) skill categories have been fully retrieved. Counter Skills is 19/25. Strike Supers, Ki Blast Supers, Strike Ultimates, Ki Blast Ultimates, Other Ultimates, all four Evasive subtypes, Power Up Supers, Other Supers, Saiyan Skills, Posing Skills, and Transformations haven't been pulled yet — each is its own Fandom category page (likely dozens of entries each).
- **Individual effect descriptions**: the ~30 entries in the Verified Effects table above are a small fraction of the 400+ total skills — everything else is currently just a name inside a category, same honesty rule as [Skills-Detail.md](Skills-Detail.md): don't infer effect from name.
- **This page needs several more research passes** to actually reach "complete." Good next steps, roughly in order of value: (1) pull the remaining Evasive/Ultimate/Super subcategory lists to get full name coverage, (2) work through Fandom's individual skill pages for effect descriptions on high-usage skills first (things that show up on Tier List/meta discussion pages), (3) fill in the Saiyan Skills and Frieza Race skill categories to match Majin/Namekian.

A community-maintained "DBXV2 Abilities Spreadsheet" turned up on Google Drive during this session's research and wasn't opened yet — worth checking directly next time, since a single well-maintained spreadsheet could cover far more ground per research pass than working through Fandom's ~15 category pages one at a time.
