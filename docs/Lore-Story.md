# Lore & Story Overview

## Setting
The game is set primarily in **Age 852**, roughly two years after the events of *Dragon Ball Xenoverse*. The Time Patrol’s hub has been rebuilt as **Conton City** after Toki-Toki City was destroyed by runaway growth of time rifts and a dragon statue created by Chronoa (the Supreme Kai of Time).

Conton City is significantly larger and contains districts modeled after iconic *Dragon Ball* locations. The Time Patrol, led by Chronoa and Future Trunks (Xeno Trunks), recruits new Patrollers to correct history alterations caused by the Time Breakers (primarily Towa and Mira).

## Main Story Summary (Spoiler-Light)
A new Time Patroller (the player’s CaC) is selected by Elder Kai and Chronoa. The Patroller travels through key historical eras (Saiyan Saga, Namek/Frieza, Android/Cell, Buu, Battle of Gods, etc.) to prevent villains empowered by Demon Realm magic from permanently altering the timeline.

Major antagonists include Towa, Mira, Turles, Lord Slug, and other empowered villains. A pivotal plot point involves Towa attempting to erase the previous Future Warrior (Ace) from history by killing Shenron before a critical wish. The player restores the original timeline.

The climax involves Mira absorbing Towa and Tokitoki’s egg. With help from Goku, the player defeats Mira. The egg hatches, creating a new timeline. Conton City celebrates.

Later DLC (especially Future Saga Chapters 1–4) expands the story with Fu’s experiments, Ultra Supervillain forms, and further threats to Conton City and the timelines, culminating in 2026.
