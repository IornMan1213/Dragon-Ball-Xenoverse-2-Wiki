# Time Rifts & Distorted Time Eggs

Five major Time Rifts appear in Conton City as the story progresses. Each contains a chain of side quests. Completing the key requirements in a rift rewards a **Distorted Time Egg**.

Collecting all **five** Distorted Time Eggs and speaking to the Supreme Kai of Time unlocks the secret Unknown History content and related rewards (including access to certain forms/characters such as Super Saiyan 3 Bardock in older reports).

## Approximate Requirements per Rift (Community-Verified)
- **Hercule / Satan House**: Complete the Great Saiyaman (and related) quests, then talk to Hercule.
- **Capsule Corporation**: Complete Vegeta’s sparring / training missions (level requirements apply, often around 40–50 for advanced), then talk to Bulma.
- **Guru’s House**: Complete a series of defense / Nail missions (often cited as 10–15 successful defenses), then talk to Guru. This rift is also critical for attribute cap raises and extra Shenron wishes.
- **Buu’s House**: Feed Majin Buu enough food to create family members (commonly 5–6, more if Majin race), then talk to Buu. Majin race players can also unlock Kid Buu transformation here.
- **Frieza’s Ship**: Progress through the Frieza Force missions (Zarbon, Ginyu, Frieza, etc.) until advanced forms appear, then obtain the egg from the relevant NPC.
