# Advancement Tests & Potential Unleashed

Advancement Tests are taken at the Patrol Academy (robot near Elder Kai). They unlock higher mentor access and culminate in the **Potential Unleashed** Awoken Skill.

## Rank Progression
Easy → Intermediate → Advanced → Kai → God → **Super** (final)

- Tests unlock through a combination of mentor lessons, story progress, and leveling.
- To unlock the final **Super Class** test, you must earn a **Z-Rank** on all previous tests (Easy through God).
- Clearing the Super Class test awards **Potential Unleashed** (Z-Rank on the final test itself is not strictly required).

## Tips for Z-Rank
Common high-value criteria that help reach Z-Rank:
- Clear quickly (often under 3 minutes is a strong target)
- High remaining health (80%+)
- Finish the last enemy with an Ultimate Attack
- Be transformed when finishing
- Land combos, grabs/throws, and maintain good accuracy
- Defeat multiple enemies / strong enemies
