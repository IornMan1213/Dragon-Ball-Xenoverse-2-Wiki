# TP Medal Farming Efficiency Comparison

This page compares the main methods for farming TP Medals in *Dragon Ball Xenoverse 2* based on community consensus (2024–2026).

## Quick Ranking (Best to Situational)

1. **Online Expert Mission Tours (EM 16 / 17 / 18)** — Highest sustained efficiency  
2. **Solo / Offline Expert Missions 16–18** — Best offline option  
3. **Shenron Wish (“I want medals!”)** — Strong burst but limited by Dragon Ball farming  
4. **Daily Missions + Login + Side Quests** — Steady passive income  
5. **First-time Parallel Quests** — One-time only, not repeatable farming  
6. **Raids / Events** — High when available, inconsistent otherwise

## Key Efficiency Insights

- **Online Expert Mission Tours** (especially cycling EM 16, 17, and 18) are the current meta for maximum TP Medals per hour.
- EM 16 and 17 are preferred for pure speed and consistency. EM 18 offers more medals per clear if your group/build can finish it quickly.
- Online multiplayer significantly increases medal rewards compared to offline (often +5 to +10 or more per run).
- Double TP Medal campaigns dramatically improve all Expert Mission methods — prioritize farming during these periods.
- Shenron wishes provide a strong alternative or supplement but are gated by Dragon Ball collection speed (PQ 23 is the usual route).
- First-time Parallel Quest clears give medals only once and are not suitable for sustained farming.

## Recommended Strategy
1. Unlock Expert Missions 16–18.
2. Farm them online in tours whenever possible.
3. Use score/EXP Super Souls if you also want experience.
4. During double medal campaigns, focus almost exclusively on Expert Missions.
5. Supplement with Shenron wishes and daily rewards.
