# Development History

*Dragon Ball Xenoverse 2* was developed by **Dimps** and published by **Bandai Namco Entertainment**. It is the direct sequel to *Dragon Ball Xenoverse* (2015).

## Key Facts
- **Announcement**: Teased May 16, 2016; fully announced May 17, 2016.
- **Release**:
  - PlayStation 4 / Xbox One: October 25–28, 2016 (region-dependent)
  - PC (Steam): October 27, 2016
  - Nintendo Switch: September 2017
  - Stadia: December 2019
  - PS5 / Xbox Series X|S (and Japanese Xbox One): May 2024
- **Directors**: Yuka Kobayashi, Takeshi Sakamoto
- **Producers**: Masayuki Hirano, Masahiro Kashiro (and others)
- Conton City was designed to be approximately **seven times larger** than Toki-Toki City from the first game.
- Target of 60 FPS on launch platforms.
- Open and closed betas were held on PlayStation 4 in October 2016.

## Design Philosophy
Producer Masayuki Hirano emphasized letting players insert themselves into the *Dragon Ball* world as the hero rather than simply controlling existing characters. The team avoided marketing the player character as a generic “avatar.”

The game shares setting DNA with *Dragon Ball Online* (the unreleased MMORPG supervised by Akira Toriyama) and continues the Time Patrol concept introduced in the first *Xenoverse*.

Long-term support far exceeded the original one-year plan, leading to nearly a decade of DLC, free updates, and the Future Saga arc concluding in 2026.
