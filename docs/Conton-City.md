# Conton City – Key Locations & NPCs

Conton City is the large hub (significantly bigger than Toki-Toki City from the first game). Use the map markers and Transfer Shop robots (red dots) for fast travel once unlocked.

## Important NPCs & Systems
- **Tosok** (near Mixing Shop / Reception area) — Pays to level up your CaC. Zeni up to level 80; TP Medals (or STP) afterward. Essential for efficient leveling to high caps.
- **Nit** — Gives the “40 ton weights” Super Soul (strong XP boost) after you have the flying license (post-Frieza Saga). Usually found above/near the reception / Parallel Quest area.
- **Guru / Nail** (Guru’s House Time Rift) — Critical for raising per-stat attribute caps (100 → 125), expanding Shenron wishes, and Namekian-related content.
- **Whis** — Mentors and later level-cap food items (Parfait, etc.). Location often in Bamboo district / near Grandpa Gohan’s hut area once unlocked.
- **Beerus** — Mentor, frequently on the roof of Orange Star High School recreation.
- **Shops (Recreation Plaza)**: Item Shop (Super Souls, capsules), Skill Shop, Clothing Shop, Mixing Shop (QQ Bangs are crafted at Capsule Corp Clothing Mixing Shop), TP Medal Shop (rotating stock, weekends traditionally strong).
- **Time Nest / Supreme Kai of Time** — Story, Distorted Time Eggs turn-in, Extra Missions for DLC sagas.
- **Patrol Academy** — Advancement Tests (robot near Elder Kai).

Mentors appear across the city (pink markers once available). Time Rifts (five major ones) contain the Distorted Time Egg questlines.
