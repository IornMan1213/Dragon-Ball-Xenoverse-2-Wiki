# Conton City

Conton City is the central hub. It is significantly larger than Toki-Toki City and contains all major systems.

## Key Systems & Areas
- **Recreation Plaza**: Item Shop, Skill Shop, Clothing Shop, Mixing Shop, TP Medal Shop
- **Patroller Academy / Business District**: Elder Kai, Advancement Tests, mentors
- **Time Nest / Time Vault**: Main story missions
- **Time Rifts**: Five race-associated rifts for Distorted Time Eggs and side content
- **Transfer Shop Robots**: Free teleportation (red map markers)
- **Hero Colosseum** and **Expert Mission** terminals

## Flying License
Required to fly freely in Conton City.

**How to Unlock**: Complete the Frieza Saga in the main story (specifically after the mission involving Frieza and Cooler — “The Final Battle! - Two Powers Fade”). The Supreme Kai of Time awards the license.

Once obtained, jump and use the fly input to soar around the city. Flight is restricted in certain interior areas.
