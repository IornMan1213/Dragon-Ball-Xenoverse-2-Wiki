# Conton City

Conton City is the central hub of *Dragon Ball Xenoverse 2*. It is significantly larger than Toki-Toki City from the first game and serves as the base for all major systems.

## Key Areas & Systems

**Recreation Plaza**  
Main shopping district. Contains:
- Item Shop (capsules, Super Souls, food, materials)
- Skill Shop
- Clothing / Equipment Shop
- Mixing Shop (for QQ Bang materials and mixing)
- TP Medal Shop (weekend rotation of rare items, skills, Super Souls)

**Business District / Patroller Academy**  
Home of Elder Kai, Advancement Tests, and mentor-related activities. Accessory Shop is also nearby.

**Time Nest / Time Vault**  
Access point for main story Time Patrol missions. Replayable later.

**Time Rifts**  
Five race-associated rifts (Hercule’s House, Capsule Corp, Guru’s House, Majin Buu’s House, Frieza’s Spaceship). Used for Distorted Time Eggs, side content, and race-specific rewards.

**Transfer Shop Robots**  
Red map markers that allow free teleportation across Conton City once activated.

**Hero Colosseum**  
Figure battler mode (separate from standard combat).

**Expert Mission Terminal**  
Access to Expert Missions for TP Medals and high-difficulty co-op content.

## Other Notes
- Flight requires a license (unlocked through story progression).
- Mentors are scattered throughout the city and can be selected as active instructors.
- Various NPCs offer side interactions, food, gestures, or small rewards.
