# Mentors (Instructors)

There are approximately 33 mentors including DLC. Each offers an initiation + lessons that reward skills. Completing lessons builds Friendship (important for Dual Ultimates, gifts, nicknames, and special training such as Beast).

**Friendship mechanics**: Raise by taking the mentor as instructor, bringing them on Parallel Quests / Extra Story, talking to them, and wearing related costumes. Max Friendship unlocks Dual Ultimate and Partner Gauge. Check progress via Lobby Menu → Play Data → Instructor Information.

## Core Mentors (Base Game Progression Order)

**Very Early (after first story missions)**  
- **Krillin** – Unlock: Complete “Make Way, Rookie Time Patroller Here!”  
  Rewards: Rise to Action, Orin Combo, Destructo-Disc, Scatter Kamehameha + Dual  
- **Tien**, **Yamcha**, **Piccolo** – Same early window / after Krillin’s first lesson  
  (Piccolo gives Evil Explosion, Super Explosive Wave evasive, Light Grenade, Special Beam Cannon + Dual)

**After Saiyan / early Namek progress + Beginner Advancement Test**  
- Raditz, Kid Gohan, Nappa, Vegeta

**After Frieza Saga + Intermediate Advancement**  
- Zarbon, Dodoria (also defeat him in Conton City), Captain Ginyu, Frieza (1st Form), Cooler (Final Form)

**After Cell / Future Trunks Saga + Advanced Advancement**  
- Android 18, Android 16, Cell (Perfect), Lord Slug

**Later (Buu Saga / God of Destruction content + higher requirements)**  
- Majin Buu, Hercule (applaud in front of him), Gohan (Adult) + Videl (complete Great Saiyaman Time Miniature quests), Gotenks, Turles, Broly (Buu Saga + God rank + find him near PQ area), Beerus, Pan, Goku, Whis (often requires Mentors DLC + God Class Advancement), and others.

## DLC Mentors
Hit and many others added across Super Packs, Legendary Packs, Conton City Vote, Hero of Justice, Future Saga, etc. Exact rewards and conditions vary by pack.

**Tip**: Complete lessons even if you switch mentors — skill rewards are permanent.
