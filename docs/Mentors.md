# Mentors (Instructors) – Full List

There are approximately 33 mentors including DLC. Each offers an initiation + up to 3 lessons that reward skills. Completing lessons also builds Friendship (important for Dual Ultimates, gifts, nicknames, and special training like Beast).

**How Friendship works**
- Raise by: taking the mentor as your current instructor, bringing them on Parallel Quests / Extra Story, talking to them, wearing related costumes.
- Max Friendship unlocks Dual Ultimate and a Partner Gauge.
- Check progress: Lobby Menu → Play Data → Instructor Information.

## Core Mentors (Base Game Progression)

**Early (after first story missions)**  
- **Krillin** – Unlock: Complete “Make Way, Rookie Time Patroller Here!”  
  Rewards: Rise to Action, Orin Combo, Destructo-Disc, Scatter Kamehameha + Dual  
- **Tien** – Same early unlock  
  Rewards: Dodon Ray, Volleyball Fist, Tri-Beam, Neo Tri-Beam + Dual  
- **Yamcha** – Same  
  Rewards: Fake Death, Wolf Fang Fist, Ki Blast Thrust, Spirit Ball + Dual  
- **Piccolo** – Same / after Krillin’s first lesson  
  Rewards: Evil Explosion, Super Explosive Wave (Evasive), Light Grenade, Special Beam Cannon + Dual  

**After Saiyan / early Namek progress + Beginner Advancement Test**  
- Raditz, Kid Gohan, Nappa, Vegeta  

**After Frieza Saga progress + Intermediate Advancement**  
- Zarbon, Dodoria (also defeat him in Conton), Captain Ginyu, Frieza (1st Form), Cooler (Final Form)  

**After Cell / Future Trunks Saga + Advanced Advancement**  
- Android 18, Android 16, Cell (Perfect), Lord Slug  

**Later (Buu Saga / God of Destruction content + higher Advancement / level requirements)**  
- Majin Buu, Hercule (applaud in front of him), Gohan (Adult) + Videl (complete Great Saiyaman Time Miniature quests), Gotenks, Turles, Broly (special conditions: Buu Saga + God rank + find him flying near PQ area), Beerus, Pan, Goku, Whis (often requires Mentors DLC pack + God Class Advancement), etc.

## DLC Mentors
Examples: Hit (Super Pack 1 + God Class Advancement), and many more added across Super Packs, Legendary Packs, Conton City Vote, Hero of Justice, Future Saga, etc. Check the Fandom Mentors page for the complete up-to-date list of rewards, as new ones continue to appear with DLC.

**Tip**: Always complete lessons even if you don’t keep the mentor as your active instructor — the skill rewards are permanent.
