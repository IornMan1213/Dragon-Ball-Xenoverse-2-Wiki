# Mentors (Instructors)

Mentors teach skills through a series of lessons (usually an initiation + three training missions). Completing lessons awards specific skills and raises friendship, which can unlock Dual Ultimates.

There are over 30 mentors in the full game (base + DLC).

## Early Mentors (Available Soon After Starting)
**Krillin**  
Unlock: Complete the first story mission (Raditz).  
Skills: Rise to Action, Orin Combo, Destructo-Disc, Scatter Kamehameha (+ Dual)

**Tien**  
Unlock: After starting Krillin’s lessons.  
Skills: Dodon Ray, Volleyball Fist, Tri-Beam, Neo Tri-Beam (+ Dual)

**Yamcha**  
Unlock: After starting Krillin’s lessons.  
Skills: Fake Death, Wolf Fang Fist, Ki Blast Thrust, Spirit Ball (+ Dual)

**Piccolo**  
Unlock: After starting Krillin’s lessons.  
Skills: Evil Explosion, Super Explosive Wave (Evasive), Light Grenade, Special Beam Cannon (+ Dual)

## Mid-Game Mentors (Saiyan / Frieza Saga Progression)
Examples include Raditz, Kid Gohan, Nappa, Vegeta, Zarbon, Dodoria, Captain Ginyu, Frieza, and others. Each follows the same lesson structure and awards a mix of Strike, Ki Blast, and Ultimate skills tied to their fighting style.

## Later Mentors
Android 18, Cell, Lord Slug, Majin Buu, Hercule, Gotenks, Beerus, Goku, Gohan & Videl, Broly, and additional DLC mentors (Hit, Whis, Cooler, Bardock, Future Gohan, Android 16, etc.).

DLC mentors generally unlock after purchasing the relevant pack and meeting story or Advancement Test requirements.

## Tips
- Complete all four lessons with a mentor to maximize rewards and friendship.
- Take the mentor into Parallel Quests to raise the friendship bar faster for Dual Ultimates.
- Mentor skills are guaranteed once the corresponding lesson is cleared — one of the most reliable ways to obtain specific moves.
