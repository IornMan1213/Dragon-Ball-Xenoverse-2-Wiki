# FUTURE SAGA Chapter 4 Research

This document records the final DLC release as a source-backed research layer. It intentionally separates confirmed DLC contents from unresolved acquisition and gameplay details.

## Release

**FUTURE SAGA Chapter 4** released July 8, 2026 on console storefronts; the Steam listing shows July 7, 2026. The platform date difference is preserved rather than normalized away.

Official Bandai Namco source: https://www.bandainamcoent.com/news/dragon-ball-xenoverse-2-final-dlc-future-saga-chapter-4-is-available-now
Official Dragon Ball source: https://en.dragon-ball-official.com/news/01_4313.html
Steam: https://store.steampowered.com/app/3905260/_2___4/

## Confirmed playable characters

| Character | Status | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Goku (Ultra Supervillain Quelled) | indexed | Official Bandai Namco and platform listings |
| Supreme Kai of Time (Ultra Supervillain) | indexed | Official Bandai Namco and platform listings |

These are separate playable character identities for the character database. They should not be silently converted into ordinary Goku/Supreme Kai of Time presets.

## Confirmed DLC contents

The official/platform listings consistently identify:

- 1 Extra Mission
- 2 Parallel Quests
- 1 Ultra Time Patrol Battle challenge category
- 4 new moves, including 1 Awoken Skill
- 6 costumes/accessories
- 4 Super Souls
- 8 loading-screen illustrations
- 1 stage
- Gallery of Time

Some content is obtained only after meeting in-game conditions; the storefront descriptions do not establish every individual unlock route.

## Gallery of Time

The new Gallery of Time feature is documented as containing more than 1,000 loading-screen illustrations and Time Patrol movies. This is a feature/database category in its own right and should not be reduced to a simple collectible count until the actual gallery inventory is catalogued.

## Ultra Time Patrol Battles

Chapter 4 adds a new high-difficulty challenge category called **Ultra Time Patrol Battles**. Official descriptions state that numerous classic characters appear in these battles. Exact battle IDs, enemy formations, rewards, drop tables, difficulty behavior, and repeat-farming rules remain unresolved here.

## Research rules

- Do not infer the four moves from trailers or character appearance alone.
- Do not assign costumes/accessories to characters without item-level evidence.
- Do not infer Super Soul effects from promotional descriptions.
- Do not treat DLC ownership as equivalent to completing an in-game unlock condition.
- Do not infer Parallel Quest reward tables from older DLC patterns.
- Preserve platform release-date differences.
- Preserve the distinction between the final DLC and the Future Saga Pack Set, which bundles Chapters 1–4.
