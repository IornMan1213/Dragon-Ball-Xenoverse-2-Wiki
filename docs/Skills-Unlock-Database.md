# Skill Unlock Database

This page documents **how skills are acquired**, separately from the raw category index. The database uses a verification-first model: a skill can be indexed from a category before its unlock route, cost, or mechanics are promoted to verified data.

## Acquisition channels

| Source | What it commonly provides | Database fields to verify |
|---|---|---|
| Mentor / instructor | Signature Super and Ultimate attacks | Mentor, friendship requirement, lesson/order |
| Parallel Quest | Rare Supers, Ultimates and Evasives | PQ number, Ultimate Finish, drop condition |
| Expert Mission | Selected high-end skills and rewards | Mission number, reward pool, conditions |
| Advancement Test | Progression and Potential Unleashed | Test number, required rank, reward |
| Time Rift | Race-specific Awoken Skills | Rift, race, prerequisite progression |
| TP Medal / skill shops | Rotating or progression-gated skills | Shop, currency, availability condition |
| DLC / Future Saga content | DLC-specific skills and transformations | DLC/Saga requirement |

## Verified starter records

### Turn Golden

- **Class:** Awoken
- **Restriction:** Frieza Race CaC
- **Unlock:** Progress the Frieza's Spaceship Time Rift and complete the required Frieza/Frieza Force progression until Frieza and Cooler training awards the transformation.
- **Ki cost:** 300
- **Database status:** Verified

### Potential Unleashed

- **Class:** Awoken
- **Restriction:** CaC
- **Unlock:** Z-rank the first five Advancement Tests and complete the sixth/final test.
- **Ki cost:** 500
- **Database status:** Verified

### Super Saiyan God Super Saiyan

- **Class:** Awoken
- **Restriction:** Saiyan CaC
- **Unlock:** Level 90+ and maximum Friendship with Whis; Whis awards the skill.
- **Ki cost:** 500
- **Database status:** Verified

### Super Saiyan God Super Saiyan (Evolved)

- **Class:** Awoken
- **Restriction:** Saiyan CaC
- **Unlock:** Obtain Super Saiyan God Super Saiyan, reach level 95+, and max Friendship with Vegeta; Whis awards the skill.
- **Ki cost:** 500
- **Database status:** Verified

## Research standard

A record is **indexed** when its name/category membership is established from the source taxonomy. It becomes **verified** only after the relevant unlock/mechanics page or another reliable reference supports the specific field.

Do not treat community build advice, damage rankings, or PvP meta claims as canonical game data without an explicit source and version context.

## Primary source

The master taxonomy is the [Dragon Ball Xenoverse 2 Wiki Skills category](https://dbxv2.fandom.com/wiki/Category:Skills). The interactive database and machine-readable JSON are the canonical project interfaces for this research.
