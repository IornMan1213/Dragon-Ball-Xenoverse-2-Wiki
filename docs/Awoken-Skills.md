# Awoken Skills (Transformations)

Awoken Skills are the transformation system. Most are race-restricted or require specific training.

## Common Unlock Paths
- **Super Saiyan / SS2 / SS3** — Saiyan race Time Rift or story-related.
- **Super Vegeta / Super Vegeta 2** — Similar, Vegeta-focused training.
- **Future Super Saiyan** — Specific conditions (often Trunks-related).
- **Potential Unleashed** — Complete and Z-Rank all Advancement Tests up to God Class, then the final test.
- **Kaioken series** — Training / specific mentors or PQs.
- **Turn Golden / Golden Evolution** — Frieza Race.
- **Become Giant / Super Namekian** — Namekian.
- **Purification** — Majin.
- **Power Pole Pro** — Earthling / specific.
- **SSGSS (Super Saiyan Blue)** and **SSB Evolution** — Late game / DLC training (Whis, etc.).
- **Ultra Instinct (Sign)** — Defeat Full Power Jiren in the relevant mission/update path.
- **Beast** — Post-DLC 16: Max friendship with Piccolo, Gohan, Videl → special training vs Cell Max. Available to all races after the update.
- **Power to Overcome** (Future Saga Chapter 4) — Clear the final DLC story missions (e.g., mission 31).

Many have ki costs, timers (some removed or adjusted in later patches), and unique properties (auto-recovery, damage multipliers, etc.).

Full details and exact conditions: Check Fandom wiki Transformation / Awoken Skill pages and the 2026 guides on dragonballxenoverse2.wiki.
