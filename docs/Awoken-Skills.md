# Awoken Skills (Transformations)

One Awoken Skill slot per CaC. Most require a minimum Ki amount to activate (some consume Ki on activation). Stages can often be skipped if you have enough Ki.

## Available to All Races
- **Kaioken** (x1 / x3 / x20)
- **Potential Unleashed** – Complete and Z-Rank Easy → Intermediate → Advanced → Kai → God Class Advancement Tests, then the final test.
- **Beast** – See detailed steps below.
- **Ultra Instinct** (Sign / full variants depending on update) – Defeat Full Power Jiren in the relevant mission path after the update that added it.
- **The Power to Overcome** – Future Saga Chapter 4 exclusive (see below).

## Race-Restricted Highlights
- **Saiyan**: Super Saiyan 1/2/3, Super Vegeta 1/2, Future Super Saiyan, SSGSS / Blue Evolution paths (Whis training / late content).
- **Frieza Race**: Turn Golden / Golden Evolution.
- **Namekian**: Become Giant / Super Namekian.
- **Majin**: Purification.
- **Earthling**: Power Pole Pro and strong Kaioken synergy.

## Exact Unlock: Beast (All Races)
1. Own the relevant free update / Hero of Justice content that introduced Beast (DLC 16 era).
2. Max Friendship with **Piccolo** *and* **Gohan & Videl** (treat them as one mentor unit).
3. Talk to Piccolo (usually near Time Nest entrance or Orange Star High School area) → select “I want to receive further training” / “I want to train even more” → “Let’s spar”.
4. Complete the special mission (fight Cell Max; Orange Piccolo and Gohan (Super Hero) assist). When allies fall, a cutscene triggers Beast on your CaC.
5. Finish the mission by defeating Cell Max → Beast is permanently unlocked for **all** your CaCs.

Beast properties (high level): Large power boost, improved Ki recovery, new combo string (H, L, H, H, H, H, H), teleport on certain basics, red eyes / hair change where applicable. Consumes 500 Ki on activation.

## Exact Unlock: The Power to Overcome (Final Awoken)
1. Purchase and install **Future Saga Chapter 4** DLC.
2. Play through the entire Future Saga Chapter 4 story (Extra Missions starting around Quest 24).
3. Complete the final mission: **Quest No. 31 – Ultimate All-Out Showdown**.
4. The Awoken Skill unlocks automatically upon clear (along with related costume rewards).

Properties (summary): Throw becomes unblockable slash; Auto-Recovery activates at ≤50% HP; can re-input for a temporary “Unleashed” mode (big damage + Ki recovery at the cost of draining HP). Consumes 500 Ki on activation. One of the most unique final forms in the game.

For full mechanical details, ki costs, and stage differences, cross-reference the Fandom Awoken Skill pages and the 2026 guides on dragonballxenoverse2.wiki.
