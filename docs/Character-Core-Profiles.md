# Character Core Profiles

This page is the first hard-written pass over the character record layer. It deliberately starts with highly connected characters whose records touch mentors, presets, skills, transformations, quests, DLC, equipment, and partner customization.

**Research state:** These entries are `partially_verified` unless explicitly marked otherwise. A sourced field is not treated as proof of every other field. Historical/current distinctions and unresolved acquisition details remain visible instead of being inferred.

## Goku

**Canonical identity:** Goku  
**Race:** Saiyan  
**Affiliation:** Universe 7; Z-Fighters  
**Mentor:** Yes  
**Related records:** Goku (GT), Goku (Super Saiyan God), SSGSS Goku, Goku (Super Saiyan 4), Goku (Ultra Instinct -Sign-), Goku (Ultra Instinct), Goku (Mini)

Goku is both a playable character and a mentor. The character record documents a large preset family rather than treating every transformation as a separate copy of the base record. The source character page reports 19 presets in the main slot including the customizable mentor slot, a separate Super Saiyan God slot, and four additional Super Saiyan Blue presets, for 24 reported presets in total.

### Mentor and customization

Goku is an available mentor. The source record also documents Festival of Universes camaraderie as a route for an additional Goku preset. This is distinct from ordinary mentor friendship and should not be merged with the mentor-training system.

### Gameplay record

The source describes Goku as having a teleport-oriented combo option, a strong backhit hitbox, and a broad mentor customization pool. It specifically documents Super Spirit Bomb as a notable character-specific combo interaction: Goku can combo into it through a heavy-break route in a way other characters cannot reproduce according to the source.

The gameplay claims above are preserved as source-reported behavior; they are not presented as a universal frame-data or tournament conclusion.

### Cross-links to research

- Skills: Kamehameha-family moves, Spirit Bomb/Super Spirit Bomb, Dragon Fist and other Goku-associated skills.
- Transformations/forms: Super Saiyan God, SSGSS, Ultra Instinct -Sign-, Ultra Instinct, Super Saiyan 4 and Goku (Mini).
- Systems: Mentor training, Festival of Universes camaraderie, Partner Customization.
- Quests: Goku-related story/PQ appearances and any quest-specific reward routes must be reconciled through the mission databases rather than inferred from the roster record.

**Verification:** `partially_verified` — identity, race, mentor status, related forms, preset structure and selected gameplay observations are sourced; a complete preset-by-preset acquisition and historical-version audit remains open.

## Vegeta

**Canonical identity:** Vegeta  
**Race:** Saiyan  
**Affiliation:** Universe 7; Dragon Team; formerly Frieza Force  
**Mentor:** Yes  
**Related records:** Prince of Destruction Vegeta, Vegeta (Super Saiyan God), Vegeta (Super Saiyan God) Ultra Supervillain, SSGSS Vegeta, SSGSS Vegeta (Evolved), Vegeta (Super Saiyan 4), Vegeta (GT), SS3 Vegeta (DAIMA)

Vegeta is a base playable character and mentor with multiple separately represented forms and variants. The canonical database must retain these variants as separate records because the source treats them as distinct gameplay identities.

### Presets and associated skills

The source character page documents multiple Battle Suit presets. Examples include a Battle Suit preset using Meteor Strike, Galick Gun, Spirit Boost, Charged Ki Wave, Super Galick Gun and Spread Shot Retreat, and another using Sledgehammer, Energy Wave Combo, Shine Shot, Energy Charge, Full Power Energy Wave and Explosive Wave. These are preset loadouts, not a claim that every listed skill is exclusive to Vegeta.

### Transformations and partner records

Vegeta's transformation family is represented separately in the canonical roster. Super Saiyan 4 has a dedicated record and a documented Customization Key 2 partner route. Super Saiyan God is also separately represented and has a documented Customization Key 18 partner route. Those keys belong to Partner Customization and must not be confused with character-unlock requirements.

### Cross-links to research

- Skills: Galick Gun, Super Galick Gun, Final Flash and other Vegeta-associated skills.
- Transformations/forms: Super Saiyan, Super Vegeta, Super Saiyan God, SSGSS, SSGSS Evolved, Super Saiyan 4 and later separately represented variants.
- Systems: Mentor training and Partner Customization.
- Quests: Vegeta appears throughout the main story and numerous Parallel Quests; exact mission/reward relationships belong in the PQ and story databases.

**Verification:** `partially_verified` — identity, race, mentor status, related-form structure, representative preset loadouts and partner-key evidence are sourced; the full preset and historical unlock audit remains open.

## Piccolo

**Canonical identity:** Piccolo  
**Race:** Namekian  
**Affiliation:** Universe 7; Z-Fighters  
**Mentor:** Yes  
**Related records:** Piccolo (Power Awakening), Orange Piccolo

Piccolo is a playable character and an early mentor. The source character record places him near the entrance to the Time Vault and documents four mentor skills: Evil Explosion, Super Explosive Wave, Light Grenade and Special Beam Cannon.

### Preset and skill evidence

The character page documents a main preset family containing skills including Super Ki Explosion, Full Power Energy Wave, Full Power Energy Blast Volley, Special Beam Cannon, Hellzone Grenade, Reverse Mabakusenko and Mystic Flash, along with DUAL Special Beam Cannon and other movement/evasive entries in the record. These should be treated as preset data, not as proof that every move is character-exclusive.

### Partner Customization distinction

Piccolo's base record is mentor-capable, while the separately represented Power Awakening and Orange Piccolo records have their own customization/availability states. The Power Awakening record explicitly reports no Partner Customization availability, and Orange Piccolo likewise reports no Partner Customization availability. This is an important example of why forms must not inherit the base character's systems automatically.

### DLC/form relationship

Piccolo (Power Awakening) and Orange Piccolo were added with Hero of Justice Pack 2. The source records identify Power Awakening as the 40th DLC character and Orange Piccolo as the subsequent distinct playable Piccolo identity. These are DLC provenance facts, not statements that DLC ownership alone is the in-game unlock condition.

**Verification:** `partially_verified` — core identity, mentor status, mentor skills, related forms and DLC provenance are sourced; complete preset, quest, unlock and reward reconciliation remains open.

## Gohan (Adult)

**Canonical identity:** Gohan (Adult)  
**Race:** Half Human-type Earthling / half Saiyan  
**Affiliation:** Universe 7; Z-Fighters  
**Mentor:** Yes  
**Related records:** Gohan (Kid), Gohan (Teen), Gohan (Future), Great Saiyaman, Gohan (DBS Super Hero), Gohan (Beast)

Gohan (Adult) represents the Buu Saga-era and later adult version of Gohan in the character database. He is both playable and mentor-capable, and the source record associates him with Videl in the mentor system.

### Preset structure

The source reports four presets on the main slot, including a customizable mentor slot, plus a separate Great Saiyaman slot. The documented presets include Turtle Hermit Gi, Uniform and Tracksuit variants. The source also records different Awoken states among the presets, including Potential Unleashed and Super Saiyan.

Representative documented skills include Burst Rush, Kamehameha, Ki Blast Cannon, Charged Ki Wave, Super Kamehameha, Spirit Explosion and Potential Unleashed. Another preset uses Meteor Blow, Kamehameha, Afterimage, Maximum Charge, Super Kamehameha and Energy Dome with Super Saiyan.

### Festival and customization research boundary

Gohan's separate Festival of Universes preset system is documented elsewhere in the source corpus. Festival camaraderie should be kept separate from ordinary mentor friendship and from DLC ownership. The record also contains several character-linked Super Souls; these should be linked to the Super Soul database rather than duplicated as character-only facts.

**Verification:** `partially_verified` — identity, race, mentor status, preset structure, representative loadouts and related-character relationships are sourced; full mentor lesson, friendship, unlock, PQ and historical preset reconciliation remains open.

## Frieza (Final Form)

**Canonical identity:** Frieza (Final Form)  
**Race:** Frieza Race  
**Affiliation:** Frieza Force; Universe 7  
**Mentor:** No  
**Partner Customization:** No  
**Related records:** Frieza (1st Form), Frieza (Full Power), Golden Frieza, Golden Frieza (Supervillain), Golden Frieza (Ultra Supervillain)

The Final Form record is intentionally separate from Frieza's other forms. The source describes this as Frieza's true form in the Namek-era sequence, while earlier forms function as power-suppressing transformations in the narrative context.

### Presets and loadouts

The source documents at least two Final Form presets. Final Form 1 uses Death Wave, Death Beam, Death Psycho Bomb, Maximum Charge, Death Meteor, Death Ball and Psychic Move, with the Super Soul `Gotcha!`. Final Form 2 substitutes Death Slash for Death Wave and uses Full Power Energy Blast Volley in the ultimate slot while retaining the other core entries.

These loadouts are preserved as preset evidence. They do not establish acquisition requirements, drop rates or shop availability.

### Mentor distinction

Frieza (Final Form) is not the mentor record. The separate Frieza (1st Form) character is mentor-capable and teaches the mentor skill path. This distinction is important when linking Frieza-associated skills: a skill associated with a Frieza preset is not automatically a mentor reward.

**Verification:** `partially_verified` — identity, race, affiliations, mentor/customization status, related forms and two preset loadouts are sourced; exact unlock route, mission appearances and historical-version reconciliation remain open.

## Research conventions used on this page

- **DLC ownership is not an unlock requirement.** A DLC association is recorded separately from the actual route used to make a character selectable.
- **Preset is not identity.** A preset loadout belongs to a character record unless the source explicitly establishes a distinct character/form identity.
- **Form is not automatically a transformation skill.** Playable forms such as Super Saiyan 4 Goku and Orange Piccolo are separate character records, while CaC Awoken Skills belong to the Awoken database.
- **Partner Customization is separate from playability.** A character can be playable without being a customizable partner, and a partner key does not by itself prove how the base character is unlocked.
- **Historical evidence remains historical.** Festival, DLC-release and older preset observations are not silently rewritten as current shop/rotation facts.
- **Unknown is better than guessed.** Missing unlock conditions, drop rates, costs, frame data and restrictions remain open research until sourced.
