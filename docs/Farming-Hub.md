# Farming Hub – Efficient Resource Gathering

Consolidated high-value methods for the most important currencies and items.

## TP Medals
- **Primary**: Expert Missions 16 & 17 (fast clears, ~16 medals each). EM 18 for slightly more + crystals.
- Shenron “I want medals!” (repeatable).
- Daily missions, events, first-clear PQs, Krillin/Yamcha repeatable quests.
- Online often yields more than offline.

## Dragon Balls
- Rotate Parallel Quests that spawn Time Patrollers (PQ 23 frequently cited as fastest).
- Defeat Patrollers preferentially with supers/ultimates.
- Avoid immediately repeating the exact same PQ.

## Experience / Levels
- High-score Parallel Quests + score-boosting Super Souls (e.g. 40-ton weights from Nit).
- Expert Missions (especially while farming TP).
- Tosok (Zeni early, TP Medals later) for direct level purchases.

## Demon Realm Crystals
- Expert Missions (higher rates on later ones).
- Fu gifts from TP Medal Shop (after unlocking corresponding raid bosses).

## Skills & Super Souls
- Mentor lessons (guaranteed).
- Parallel Quests + Ultimate Finish conditions.
- TP Medal Shop rotation.
- Crystal Raids / Double Crystal Raids for broader skill access.
- Shenron skill wishes.
