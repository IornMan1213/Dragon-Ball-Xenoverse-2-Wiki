# Farming Hub – Efficient Resource Gathering

Consolidated high-value methods for the most important currencies and items.

## TP Medals
- **Primary**: Expert Missions 16 & 17 (fast clears, ~16 medals offline; more online). EM 18 for slightly higher + crystals.
- Shenron “I want medals!” (repeatable).
- Daily missions, events, first-clear PQs, Krillin/Yamcha repeatables.

## Dragon Balls
The repository's canonical farming relationship layer identifies **PQ15, PQ22, PQ44, PQ45, PQ68, PQ83, and PQ88** as Dragon Ball routes.
- Use the canonical route set as the relationship-backed inventory of documented farming PQs.
- Route efficiency, drop probability, and Ultimate Finish requirements are separate research fields and must not be inferred from the farming relationship alone.
- See the individual PQ records for the maintained objectives/reward fields.

## Experience / Levels
- High-score Parallel Quests or Expert Missions + strong score Super Souls:
  - **My Hobbies Are Reading and Sports** (×3 score)
  - **40 Ton Weights** (×2 score, easy to get from Nit)
  - Marba’s “Now you're upping the ante” (random high multipliers)
- Stack with in-game ×2/×3 XP campaigns for extreme gains.
- Later levels: farm TP Medals and buy levels from Tosok.

## Demon Realm Crystals
- Expert Missions (higher rates later).
- Fu gifts from TP Medal Shop after unlocking corresponding raid bosses.

## Skills & Super Souls
- Mentor lessons (guaranteed).
- Parallel Quests with skill relationships. Ultimate Finish requirements and reward triggers are separate fields; do not treat the existence of a PQ→skill relationship as proof of a guaranteed or Ultimate-Finish-only skill reward.
- TP Medal Shop rotation.
- Crystal Raids / Double Crystal Raids.
- Shenron skill wishes.

## Acquisition presentation contract
- A **PQ→skill relationship** establishes that the skill is associated with the quest in the canonical relationship graph; it does **not** by itself establish a guaranteed reward, an Ultimate Finish requirement, an enemy-specific trigger, or a drop probability.
- **Ultimate Finish** should be displayed only when the corresponding canonical/research field is independently evidenced. An unresolved or unknown research field must remain visibly unresolved rather than being converted to a yes/no mechanic in presentation copy.
- **Guaranteed** should be reserved for deterministic routes such as documented mentor-lesson rewards or explicitly verified guaranteed rewards. Do not use “guaranteed” as shorthand for “listed in the reward table.”
- When a research record says `unknown`, `unresolved`, or `partially_verified`, presentation pages must preserve that uncertainty and link back to the underlying record instead of silently promoting it to canonical acquisition behavior.
