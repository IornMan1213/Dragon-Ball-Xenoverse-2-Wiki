# Parallel Quests – High-Value Farming Routes

Parallel Quests (PQs) are the core side content. They are the main source of skills, equipment, Dragon Balls (via Time Patrollers), Zeni, and many character presets. Base game has 100; DLC has added dozens more.

## Best Dragon Ball Farming Routes (Community Consensus)
- **PQ 23 – The Explosion of Namek**: Frequently cited as the fastest repeatable route. Defeat Frieza quickly (under ~3 minutes), then clear the three Time Patrollers that appear. Excellent for solo farming.
- **PQ 4 – Prepare for the Attack of Saiyans!**: Beginner-friendly Dragon Ball route.
- **PQ 79 – Great Ape Festival**: Solid mid-game option that can yield multiple Patroller waves and Dragon Balls if timed well.

Tip: Rotate between different PQs rather than spamming the exact same one repeatedly to maintain better drop rates. Defeat Patrollers with supers/ultimates when possible.

## Other Notable High-Value PQs
- PQ 134 and similar late/high-difficulty PQs are often recommended for specific meta skills (charges, powerful supers) and Super Souls.
- Many DLC PQs from Future Saga chapters and earlier packs drop exclusive skills, costumes, and Super Souls. Always check the PQ description for Ultimate Finish conditions to maximize rewards and Z-Ranks.

**Ultimate Finish**: Fulfilling the hidden third condition usually yields better rewards and higher scores.
