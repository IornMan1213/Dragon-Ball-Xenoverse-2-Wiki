# Future Saga Overview (Chapters 1–4)

The Future Saga is the major late-game story expansion for *Dragon Ball Xenoverse 2*, released across four chapters culminating in July 2026.

## High-Level Summary
Fu conducts experiments with **Ultra Supervillain** states across timelines, creating escalating threats. Time Patrollers intervene across multiple chapters involving characters such as Goku Black, Vegeta (SSG Ultra Supervillain), Jiren, Belmod, Golden Frieza, Broly variants, and others.

**Chapter 4** (final) concludes the arc, introduces Goku (Ultra Supervillain Quelled) and Supreme Kai of Time (Ultra Supervillain) as playable, adds the **Power to Overcome** Awoken Skill (unlocked by completing the final story mission), new Parallel Quests, Ultra Time Patrol Battles, a Time Gallery feature, and related costumes/Super Souls. A post-credits scene teases the future (Age 1000) relevant to *Xenoverse 3*.

Each chapter typically adds:
- New playable characters (including Ultra Supervillain forms)
- Extra Mission story arcs
- New Parallel Quests
- Skills, costumes, Super Souls, and illustrations
