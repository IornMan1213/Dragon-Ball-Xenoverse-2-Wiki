# Future Saga Overview (Chapters 1–4)

The **Future Saga** is the major late-game story expansion for *Dragon Ball Xenoverse 2*, released across four chapters and culminating with Chapter 4 in July 2026.

## Chapter 4 — Final Chapter

Chapter 4 is the final FUTURE SAGA chapter and adds:

- **Goku (Ultra Supervillain Quelled)** as a playable character.
- **Supreme Kai of Time (Ultra Supervillain)** as a playable character.
- **1 Extra Mission**.
- **2 Parallel Quests**.
- **Ultra Time Patrol Battles**, a new high-difficulty challenge quest featuring classic characters.
- **4 new moves**, including **1 Awoken Skill**.
- **6 costumes/accessories**.
- **4 Super Souls**.
- **8 loading-screen illustrations**.
- **1 new stage**.
- **Gallery of Time**, which can display over 1,000 loading-screen illustrations and Time Patrol movies.

The official DLC listing describes the chapter as the culmination of Fu's Ultra Supervillain experiments and confirms that the two new characters participate in the new Extra Mission content.

### The Power to Overcome

**The Power to Overcome** is the Chapter 4 Awoken Skill for Custom Characters. The repo currently records its existence and Chapter 4 DLC requirement. Detailed mechanical values, stage behavior, and exact unlock sequencing are intentionally kept as separately sourced data rather than inferred from the official feature summary.

## Story Context

The Future Saga centers on Fu's manipulation and experimentation around altered timelines and **Ultra Supervillain** power. Across the four chapters, the Time Patrol confronts escalating changes involving characters such as Goku Black, Vegeta, Jiren, Belmod, Golden Frieza, Broly, and others.

Chapter 4 closes this major DLC storyline and adds new endgame challenge and archive content through Ultra Time Patrol Battles and the Gallery of Time.
