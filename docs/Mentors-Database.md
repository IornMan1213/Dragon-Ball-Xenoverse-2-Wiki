# Mentors Database

Mentors, Masters, or Instructors are Conton City NPCs who provide training missions and unique skills. Each mentor offers four training missions. Mentor choice also affects Challenge Quest assistance, commentary, Parallel Quest appearances, friendship progression, and some Extra Story content. citeturn0search1

## Mentor record schema

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| Mentor | Character name |
| Unlock | Requirement to make the mentor available |
| Training 1–4 | Skills/rewards from each lesson |
| Friendship | Requirements and benefits |
| Dual Ultimate | Reward associated with max friendship |
| Partner Customization | Available customization options |
| Extra Story | Mentor-specific effects, when applicable |
| DLC | Required pack, if applicable |

## Training progression

The standard mentor path is:

1. Find the mentor in Conton City.
2. Meet their availability requirement.
3. Select **Be my instructor**.
4. Complete the four training missions.
5. Increase friendship through quests and mentor interactions.
6. At maximum friendship, unlock the mentor's Dual Ultimate and partner-gauge progression. citeturn0search1

## Friendship system

Friendship can be increased by going on quests with the mentor while they are your instructor, talking to them when returning to Conton City, and using certain costumes. The current friendship level is visible through **Play Data → Instructor Information**. citeturn0search1

## Partner Gauge

After maximum friendship, the mentor gains a Partner Gauge. Its level affects the strength of the mentor's Dual Ultimate. The gauge can be raised through quests, using the Dual Ultimate, and fighting alongside the mentor in Extra Story content. citeturn0search1

## Database expansion target

The finished mentor database should include every available mentor, including DLC mentors, with:

- exact unlock conditions
- all four training missions
- each skill reward
- friendship requirements
- Dual Ultimate
- partner customization options
- gifts
- nicknames/marks
- relevant Extra Story differences

The Fandom reference currently reports **33 available mentors including DLC characters**, but the project should keep this as a versioned count rather than hard-coding it as permanently true. citeturn0search1
