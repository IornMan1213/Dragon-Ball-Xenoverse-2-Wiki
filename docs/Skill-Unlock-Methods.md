# How to Unlock (Almost) Every Skill / Move in Dragon Ball Xenoverse 2

Skills (Super Attacks, Ultimate Attacks, Evasive Skills, etc.) are obtained through several primary methods. RNG is involved for many Parallel Quest drops.

## Main Methods (Ranked by Efficiency for Most Players)

1. **Mentor / Instructor Training** (Most reliable for signature moves)
   - Talk to a mentor in Conton City (pink markers on map once unlocked).
   - Complete their initiation + lessons / school missions.
   - Rewards include unique supers, ultimates, and dual ultimates.
   - There are ~33 mentors including DLC ones. Conditions: story progress + sometimes Advancement Tests or DLC ownership.
   - Example: Krillin gives Destructo-Disc, Scatter Kamehameha, etc.

2. **Parallel Quests (PQs)** — Primary RNG source
   - Clear specific PQs (often with Z-Rank or special conditions for better drop rates).
   - Many skills drop from Time Patrollers or as clear rewards.
   - Tip: Do different PQs rather than spamming one; fight the original user of the move when possible.
   - Full lists live on the Fandom wiki under each skill page and PQ pages.

3. **TP Medal Shop (Conton City)**
   - Rotating stock of skills (updates weekly).
   - Buy with TP Medals (farm via Expert Missions, especially EM 16 / Crystal related, or high-score PQs).
   - Great for Ultra Instinct moves and many DLC skills when they appear.

4. **Skill Shop (Zeni)**
   - Sells unique moves not available elsewhere.
   - Stock expands with story progress.

5. **Shenron Wishes**
   - Collect 7 Dragon Balls (farm via PQs that spawn Time Patrollers).
   - Relevant wishes: "I want a new Super Attack", "I want a new Ultimate Attack", etc.
   - Some wishes are one-time or progressive.

6. **Advancement Tests / Potential Unleashed path**
   - Complete and Z-Rank Easy → Intermediate → Advanced → Kai → God Class tests to unlock the final test and Potential Unleashed (and related skills).

7. **Expert Missions & Raids**
   - Some exclusive drops and high-value farming.

8. **DLC Story / Extra Missions / Parallel Quests**
   - Future Saga chapters, Hero of Justice packs, Conton City Vote, DAIMA pack, etc., each add new skills with specific clear conditions.

9. **Other**
   - Some skills from story progression, specific NPCs, or one-time events (Festival of Universes auras are cosmetic).

### Tips for Completionists
- Track progress with community spreadsheets (search Reddit for current ones).
- Dual Ultimates often require specific mentor paths or high friendship.
- Many late DLC skills (Beast-related, Ultra Supervillain variants, Power to Overcome, etc.) require owning the corresponding DLC and clearing its final missions / specific PQs.
- Unobtainable by CaCs: Certain exclusive character moves (e.g., some pure Ultra Instinct Goku animations in older content).

For exhaustive per-skill tables (name, ki cost, users, exact unlock PQ/mentor), refer to:
- https://dbxv2.fandom.com/wiki/Category:Skills
- Individual skill pages on the Fandom wiki
- Burcol’s YouTube unlock-all videos (search by DLC number)

## Recommended Order for New Players
1. Finish main story + get flying license.
2. Unlock early mentors (Krillin, Yamcha, Tien, etc.).
3. Start Advancement Tests.
4. Farm TP Medals and do high-value PQs.
5. Work on race-specific Time Rift quests for Awoken Skills.
