# Skills Database (Expanded Local Reference)

This page keeps core skill information **on-wiki**. Full exhaustive tables live on Fandom; this wiki hosts categorized lists, acquisition methods, and notable examples so users can work without leaving.

## Skill Slot Structure
- 4 × Super Attacks
- 2 × Ultimate Attacks
- 1 × Evasive Skill
- 1 × Awoken Skill (transformation)

## Acquisition Methods (All Local)
| Method | Notes |
|--------|-------|
| Mentor Lessons | Guaranteed skills from each instructor’s 4 training missions |
| Parallel Quests | Many skills listed as rewards; Ultimate Finish improves results |
| TP Medal Shop | Rotating stock of skills and Super Souls |
| Shenron Wishes | Specific Super / Ultimate attacks via dedicated wishes |
| Crystal Raids / Double Crystal Raids | Broad skill pool |
| DLC Extra Missions & PQs | Exclusive skills per pack / Future Saga chapter |
| Events / Festival | Temporary or permanent rewards |

## Super Attacks – Notable Examples by Type
**Strike Supers** (close-range / physical / grabs): Orin Combo, Sledgehammer, Meteor Crash, Time Skip series (Hit), Spirit Stab, Savage Strike, Aura Slide, etc.

**Ki Blast Supers**: Kamehameha variants, Galick Gun, Destructo-Disc, Big Bang Attack, Death Beam, Consecutive Energy Blast, Dodon Ray, Tri-Beam, Masenko, Evil Explosion, Light Grenade, and many DLC additions.

**Charge / Utility**: Energy Charge, Full Power Charge, Maximum Charge, Ultimate Charge, Burst Charge, Rise to Action, Super Guard, Afterimage, etc.

## Ultimate Attacks – Notable Examples
**Strike Ultimates**: Dragon Fist, Unrelenting Barrage, Brave Sword Attack, various mentor and PQ rewards.

**Ki Blast Ultimates**: Super Kamehameha, Final Flash, Special Beam Cannon, Spirit Bomb variants, Minus Energy Power Ball, x100 Big Bang Kamehameha, and numerous DLC ultimates.

## Evasive Skills – Notable Examples
Spirit Explosion, Super Explosive Wave, Punisher Guard, Instant Rise, Super Back/Front Jump, Break Strike, various character-specific and DLC evasives (Science Vanish, Total Evasion, Evil Whirlwind, etc.).
