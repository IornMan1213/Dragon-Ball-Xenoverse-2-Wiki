# Hero Colosseum

Hero Colosseum is a board-game style minigame / figure battler accessible in Conton City (platform above Recreation Plaza / Capsule Corp area). It features its own story battles, free battles, figure collection, and capsule machines that consume TP Medals.

## Core Loop
- Collect figures of varying rarity (N / R / SR / UR).
- Build decks of 5 figures and position them strategically on a board.
- Battle AI or other players; figures have attack, defense, speed, skills, and ultimate gauges.
- Level figures, Limit Break them for higher rarity/stats, and dismantle extras for FXP / materials.
- Capsule machines (Normal, Special, Bulk, UR) are the main way to obtain new figures using TP Medals.

## Tips from Community
- Farm TP Medals (Expert Missions or Shenron) to buy capsule pulls in bulk.
- Certain figures (e.g. specific Gohans, Videl, high-end URs like Kid Buu, SSGSS forms) enable strong strategies for story and high-star battles.
- Bulk capsules with number ranges help target specific figure IDs.
- Completing story + free battles and collecting a certain number of unique figures unlocks related trophies/achievements and can reward additional content or TP.
