# DLC Overview

*Dragon Ball Xenoverse 2* has received a long-running sequence of DLC packs and free updates. The **FUTURE SAGA** is the game's final major story DLC line, spanning Chapters 1–4.

|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|

## Chapter 1

Adds five playable characters: Vegeta (Super Saiyan God, Ultra Supervillain), Goku Black (Super Saiyan Rosé, Ultra Supervillain), Broly (Restrained), Android 18 (DB Super), and Videl (DB Super). It also adds 3 Extra Missions, 12 Parallel Quests, 15 Additional Moves, 3 costumes/accessories, 5 Super Souls, and 63 illustrations.

## Chapter 2

Adds Jiren (Full Power, Ultra Supervillain), Belmod, and Goku (Mini). The official listing gives 1 Extra Mission arc, 4 Parallel Quests, 7 Additional Moves, 2 costumes/accessories, 3 Super Souls, and 14 illustrations. Bandai Namco notes that Goku (Mini)'s voice-over is Japanese-only.

## Chapter 3

Adds Golden Frieza (Ultra Supervillain) and Broly (DB Super). It contains 1 Extra Mission arc, 3 Parallel Quests, 6 Additional Moves, 5 costumes/accessories, 3 Super Souls, 23 illustrations, plus the extra scenario **Cheelai & Broly All Out! Conton City Patrol**.

## Chapter 4 — Final Chapter

Chapter 4 is the culmination of the FUTURE SAGA and adds two playable characters: **Goku (Ultra Supervillain Quelled)** and **Supreme Kai of Time (Ultra Supervillain)**.

Officially listed content includes:

- 1 Extra Mission
- 2 Parallel Quests
- 1 high-difficulty **Ultra Time Patrol Battles** quest
- 4 new moves, including 1 Awoken Skill
- 6 costumes and accessories
- 4 Super Souls
- 8 loading-screen illustrations
- 1 stage
- **Gallery of Time**

The Gallery of Time lets players view **over 1,000 loading-screen illustrations** as well as Time Patrol movies. The DLC is also described as featuring Extra Missions where the new characters play active roles.

The new Awoken Skill is **The Power to Overcome**. The wiki currently tracks its existence and DLC requirement, while detailed mechanical values and exact unlock sequencing are kept separate until independently sourced.

## Other DLC Lines

Earlier content includes the **Super Pass**, **Extra Pass**, **Ultra Packs**, **Legendary Packs**, **HERO OF JUSTICE Pack Set**, **Conton City Vote Pack**, **Legend Patrol Pack**, and other individual DLC packs. These introduced major portions of the playable roster, story content, skills, Parallel Quests, costumes, Super Souls, and systems.

For the most current official catalog and bundle information, see the [Bandai Namco Xenoverse 2 DLC page](https://en.bandainamcoent.eu/dragon-ball/dragon-ball-xenoverse-2/dlc).
