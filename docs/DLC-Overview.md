# DLC Overview (Future Saga Focus)

*Dragon Ball Xenoverse 2* received extensive DLC from 2016–2026. The final major arc is **Future Saga** (Chapters 1–4).

## Future Saga Summary
**Chapter 1**  
- New characters including Ultra Supervillain forms (Vegeta Super Saiyan God USV, Goku Black Super Saiyan Rosé USV, etc.)  
- Extra Missions, 12 Parallel Quests, additional moves, Super Souls, costumes

**Chapter 2**  
- Jiren (Full Power Ultra Supervillain), Belmod, Goku (Mini)  
- Extra Mission arc, more PQs, moves, Super Souls

**Chapter 3**  
- Golden Frieza (Ultra Supervillain), Broly (DB Super)  
- Extra Mission arc + Conton City scenario, additional content

**Chapter 4 (Final DLC – July 2026)**  
- Goku (Ultra Supervillain Quelled), Supreme Kai of Time (Ultra Supervillain)  
- New Awoken Skill: **The Power to Overcome**  
- Ultra Time Patrol Battles, additional PQs, moves, Super Souls, stage, Gallery feature  
- Marks the end of major content support

Earlier DLC lines (Super Packs, Extra Packs, Ultra Packs, Legendary Packs, Hero of Justice, etc.) added the bulk of the playable roster, skills, Parallel Quests, and systems over the years.

**Sources**: Official Bandai Namco DLC pages, Dragon Ball Official Site, Gematsu, community DLC trackers.
