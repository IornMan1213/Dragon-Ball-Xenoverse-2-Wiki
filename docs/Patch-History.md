# Major Patch & DLC History (Summary)

*Dragon Ball Xenoverse 2* received continuous support from 2016 through 2026. Below is a high-level summary of major content milestones rather than exhaustive patch notes.

## Launch & Early Support (2016–2017)
- October/November 2016: Base game release (PS4, Xbox One, PC).
- Multiple Super Pack and Extra Pack DLCs adding characters, Parallel Quests, skills, and story content.
- Nintendo Switch release in 2017.

## Mid-Cycle Expansion (2018–2023)
- Ongoing character and skill additions from *Dragon Ball Super*, movies, and GT.
- Introduction and expansion of systems such as Crystal Raids, Hero Colosseum, Partner Customization, and higher level caps over time.
- Regular free updates, balance changes, and event content (Festival of Universes, raids).
- 2023: Significant updates including difficulty options and further Awoken Skill support (Beast, etc.).

## Next-Gen & Late Support (2024–2026)
- May 2024: PS5 / Xbox Series X|S versions (and broader platform availability).
- **Future Saga** arc begins:
  - Chapter 1–3: New story, Ultra Supervillain forms, additional Parallel Quests, characters, and skills.
  - Level cap increases (eventually reaching 199).
- **Future Saga Chapter 4** (July 2026): Final major DLC.
  - New playable characters (including Goku Ultra Supervillain Quelled and Supreme Kai of Time Ultra Supervillain).
  - New Awoken Skill: The Power to Overcome.
  - Additional Parallel Quests, skills, Super Souls, stage, and Ultra Time Patrol Battles.
  - Marks the conclusion of major content support for the game.

## Key Ongoing Systems Added Over Time
- Expanded Awoken Skills (Potential Unleashed, Beast, Ultra Instinct variants, Power to Overcome, race exclusives).
- Level cap progression to 199.
- Extensive DLC Parallel Quests and Expert Mission content.
- Regular TP Medal Shop rotations, events, and quality-of-life improvements.

**Sources**: Official Bandai Namco announcements, Dragon Ball Official Site, Gematsu, Wikipedia release/DLC summaries, community patch tracking, in-game content.

This is a summary of major milestones. Full granular balance patch notes are extensive and best referenced via official patch notes when available.
