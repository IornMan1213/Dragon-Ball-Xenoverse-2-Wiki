# Expert Missions 01–15 — Individual Research Layer

This page expands the first fifteen Expert Missions from the system index into mission-level records. It is intentionally conservative: documented reward identities and encounter structure are separated from guaranteed/RNG acquisition, exact timers, drop rates, and version-sensitive mechanics that still require direct reconciliation.

**Scope:** Base-game Expert Mission tutorial

## EM15 — Extreme Malice!

**ID:** EM-15  
**Encounter:** rotating Supervillain encounters / extended boss gauntlet  
**Verification:** `partially_verified`

### Rewards

- **24,000 Zeni** documented as the basic reward in the long-standing mission guide.
- **Blue Hurricane** documented as the associated skill reward.
- Some current community references additionally associate **Marbling Drop** with EM15; this must remain an acquisition-source claim pending reconciliation with the canonical skill/reward data.
- TP Medal amount requires mission-specific verification.

### Encounter structure

Current guides describe EM15 as a multi-stage Supervillain gauntlet involving encounters such as Broly, Omega Shenron, Kid Buu, Final Form Frieza, Janemba, and a final Mira encounter, with portal-style side fights reported during the Janemba portion. These details are useful research leads but should not be treated as immutable without version-specific confirmation.

### Mechanics to capture

- boss sequence and possible variants
- portal/side-fight behavior
- Janemba stamina/teleport attacks
- giant Ki Blast interactions
- Peeler Storm and other raid-style attacks
- final Mira phase
- mission timer and failure state
- offline CPU behavior
- online player-count behavior

### Strategy evidence vs canonical data

Community guides frequently discuss healing-oriented Super Souls, Energy Zone, Super Guard, Vanisher Guard, and giant-Ki-Blast counters for EM15. These are **player strategy evidence**, not mandatory mission mechanics and must remain labeled accordingly.

### Open fields

Exact current boss sequence, health values, timers, reward layers, skill acquisition conditions, EXP, Demon Realm Crystals, rank requirements, and version differences remain research targets.

# Verification Policy

1. A listed skill is not automatically a guaranteed drop.
2. A basic Zeni reward is not automatically the complete reward table.
3. A community farming route is not a canonical game mechanic.
4. Historical guides may document an older mission state; current behavior must retain version context.
5. Conflicting reward names or amounts remain attributed rather than silently normalized.
6. Boss-only mechanics must not be inferred from the corresponding playable character.
7. Unknown values remain `null`/unverified until evidence supports them.
