# Builds, Attributes, QQ Bangs & Meta

## Attributes
Six main stats: Health, Max Ki, Max Stamina, Basic Attacks, Strike Supers, Ki Blast Supers.

- Points gained per level; total available depends on level cap (e.g., hundreds by late game).
- Cap per stat raised via Guru (to 125) then further systems.
- Race + height/weight affect base values and multipliers.
- Clothing and QQ Bangs add/subtract arrows (up to +5 / -5 typically).

## QQ Bangs
Crafted by mixing clothing at the Clothing shop / special NPCs. Goal is usually balanced or specialized +5 in desired stats with minimal negatives.

Community spreadsheets track optimal mixes for each race and playstyle (PvE raid, PvP, hybrid).

## Popular Approaches (High Level)
- **Saiyan**: Transformation heavy, flexible.
- **Earthling**: Strong basics + Power Pole / Kaioken.
- **Namekian**: Sustain / Giant Form tank.
- **Frieza Race**: Speed and Golden forms.
- **Majin**: Durability + Purification.

Super Souls are critical — many provide huge conditional bonuses (damage, recovery, ki, etc.).
