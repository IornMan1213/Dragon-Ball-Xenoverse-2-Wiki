# Builds – Race, Attributes & Loadouts

## Core Principles
- Race determines base-stat tendencies, preferred damage lanes and available Awoken Skills.
- Pick a primary damage lane: **Strike** or **Ki Blast**. Hybrid builds are possible, but specialization usually makes attribute planning easier.
- Treat **Ki and Stamina as breakpoints**, not stats you automatically max. Your QQ Bang and desired bar count change the amount you need.
- Build the loadout around a job: PvE farming, Expert Missions, raids, PvP or general story play.

## High-Level Attribute Planning
At the current high-level endgame, attribute points are valuable enough that every point should have a job. Before finalizing a build, decide:

1. Target Ki bars.
2. Target Stamina bars.
3. Primary damage stat.
4. Basic Attack investment, if the build relies on neutral/basic pressure.
5. Whether Health is worth the opportunity cost for the chosen race.

Do not copy a 199-point spread blindly. QQ Bang bonuses, race, height/weight and the purpose of the build can change the optimal distribution.

## Recommended Build Archetypes
| Archetype | Primary focus | Typical use |
|---|---|---|
| Ki Blast Glass Cannon | Ki Blast + Ki | Fast PvE/PQ/Expert Mission clears |
| Strike Rushdown | Strike + Basic | Close-range PvE and PvP |
| Stamina Tank | Stamina + survivability | Defensive PvP / long fights |
| Immortal PvE | Health/Stamina + sustain | Difficult PvE and farming |
| Balanced Story | Moderate damage + resource bars | General progression |

## QQ Bang Goals
A strong QQ Bang should support the actual build rather than chase a generic “perfect” roll. Prioritize positives in the stats you use and avoid negatives that directly undermine your damage or resource plan.

## Super Soul & Limit Burst Synergy
Choose a Super Soul by **trigger condition + actual effect + Limit Burst**, not by the headline percentage alone. A slightly smaller bonus that activates consistently can outperform a larger conditional bonus during routine farming.

## Skill Loadout Template
**Supers:** one reliable neutral/confirm tool + one damage/utility tool.  
**Ultimates:** one primary cash-out + one situational option.  
**Evasive:** prioritize stamina efficiency and the situations you actually lose to.  
**Awoken:** match the transformation to the build's damage/resource plan.  
**Super Soul:** cover the weakness your skills do not solve.
