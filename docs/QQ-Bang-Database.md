# QQ Bang Database

QQ Bangs are stat-modifying equipment created at the Capsule Corporation Clothing Mixing Shop. They override the attribute contribution of equipped clothing, allowing a build to use outfits for appearance while keeping a chosen stat profile. citeturn0search9

## Core attributes

Every QQ Bang record should track the six major CaC attributes:

- Health
- Ki
- Stamina
- Basic Attack
- Strike Supers
- Ki Blast Supers

Each attribute can receive a positive or negative modifier. citeturn0search9

## Why QQ Bangs matter

A clothing set may look ideal but have poor stats for a particular build. A QQ Bang separates **appearance** from **attribute optimization**, which is why QQ Bangs are foundational to serious CaC builds.

### Build archetype tags

The eventual searchable database should classify QQ Bangs as:

- Strike-focused
- Ki Blast-focused
- Basic Attack
- Hybrid
- Ki + Stamina
- Health + Stamina
- Glass cannon
- Defensive
- General-purpose

## Recipe research

Recipe data should be stored independently from the final stat result because multiple recipes and material combinations can matter to different players. The database should record the exact recipe, not merely say "mix high-stat clothes."

## Verification standard

A QQ Bang should be marked **verified** only when the six-stat result and recipe are confirmed. Player screenshots and community recipes can be used as research leads, but uncertain results should remain **unverified** until corroborated.
