# PQ cross-domain source notes

## Current DLC endpoint

Bandai Namco's July 8, 2026 announcement identifies FUTURE SAGA Chapter 4 as the final DLC for Dragon Ball Xenoverse 2 and says it includes Parallel Quests, additional moves, costumes and other content. The official DLC catalog likewise lists the Future Saga Pack Set and its Parallel Quest content.

## PQ 185-186

The current Steam all-PQ guide lists PQ 185 `A God's Amusement` and PQ 186 `Frieza's Right-Hand Man`, including their reward lists. These records are therefore part of the current canonical numbered range and should not be removed because of older datamined numbering models.

## Reward normalization

A reward relationship is only promoted when the source explicitly names the reward. Quest enemies, featured characters, or story participants are not automatically reward relationships.

## Farming normalization

A farming edge identifies a documented repeatable route. It does not mean the item is guaranteed on every clear, and it does not replace the underlying drop-condition record.
