# Parallel Quest population pass 6

This pass moves the PQ database beyond seed-only records into populated, source-backed quest data.

## Populated records

- PQ 1 — Being a Time Patroller
- PQ 10 — Saiyan Survivors
- PQ 11 — Burst Open and Mix!
- PQ 12 — Simian Battlefront
- PQ 13 — Namekian Dragon Balls
- PQ 14 — Saiyan Pride

Each populated record carries difficulty, unlock information, objectives, Ultimate Finish condition, enemy roster, reward pools, skill/equipment/Super Soul relationships, verification status, and source provenance where the source provides it.

## Data policy

These records are `partially_verified`, not fully verified. The source-backed fields are populated, but the wiki will not promote a quest to `verified` until its complete objective, Ultimate Finish, enemy, reward, acquisition, and unlock data has been independently reconciled.

PQ 36 remains intentionally absent because the current evidence treats it as a numbering gap rather than a real quest.
