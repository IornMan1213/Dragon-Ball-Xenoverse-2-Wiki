# Future Saga source note

Bandai Namco's current official material identifies FUTURE SAGA Chapter 4 as the final DLC for DRAGON BALL XENOVERSE 2. The publisher lists two new playable characters, Ultra Time Patrol Battles, Time Gallery, the final Extra Missions Arc, Parallel Quests, additional moves and costumes.

This is an official-content baseline only. The wiki still requires record-level verification for individual missions, rewards, skills, costumes and mechanics.
