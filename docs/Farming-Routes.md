# Farming Routes (Expanded)

Practical, high-efficiency routes for common resources.

## Dragon Balls
**Primary Route – PQ 23 (The Explosion of Namek)**  
- Defeat Frieza under ~3 minutes.  
- Clear the three Time Patrollers that appear.  
- Fastest and most consistent solo route.  

**Alternatives**  
- PQ 4 (early-game friendly)  
- PQ 79 (Great Ape Festival – more Patroller waves, longer)

## TP Medals
**Best Overall**  
Online Expert Mission Tours cycling **EM 16 (SSGSS Vegeta)**, **EM 17 (SSGSS Goku)**, and **EM 18 (Broly Supervillain)**.

**Offline**  
Repeat EM 16/17 (16 medals) or EM 18 (18 medals).

See the dedicated [TP Medal Farming Comparison](TP-Medal-Farming-Comparison.md) page for full efficiency details.

## Experience / Leveling
- Early/mid: Parallel Quests + story  
- Late game (especially to 199): Expert Missions 16–18 with score-boosting Super Souls + buy levels from Tosok using TP Medals  
- Stack with in-game EXP campaigns when available

## Skills & Super Souls
- Mentor lessons (guaranteed)  
- High-value PQs with Ultimate Finish  
- TP Medal Shop rotations  
- Crystal Raids and Expert Missions

## Materials / QQ Bang Crafting
- Mixing Shop materials from PQs, Expert Missions, and Conton City collection  
- Crystal Raids for volume
