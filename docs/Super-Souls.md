# Super Souls Overview

Super Souls are equippable passive items that grant a wide variety of effects (damage boosts, recovery, ki/stamina modifications, conditional triggers, Limit Burst effects, etc.). They are one of the most important customization layers after attributes and skills.

## How to Obtain
- Parallel Quest drops
- Mentor / friendship rewards and gifts
- TP Medal Shop and other shops
- Event / Festival rewards
- Some story and Extra Mission clears
- Specific high-value PQs and Expert Missions

Many powerful Super Souls are tied to specific mentors, characters, or late-game content.

## Usage Notes
- Only one Super Soul can be equipped at a time on a CaC.
- Effects range from simple (small permanent boosts) to highly conditional (on transformation, on low HP, on just guard, after certain actions, etc.).
- Some of the strongest souls for PvE farming, raids, or PvP are community staples and appear frequently in build guides.
