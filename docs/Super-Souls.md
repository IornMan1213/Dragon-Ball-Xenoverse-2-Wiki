# Super Souls – Farming & Acquisition Guide

Super Souls are equippable items that provide conditional or always-on effects. They are essential for builds, Limit Burst synergy, and efficient leveling.

## Primary Ways to Obtain Super Souls

### 1. TP Medal Shop (Most Reliable for Many Souls)
- Located in the Recreation Plaza.
- Stock rotates regularly (typically weekly).
- Frequently sells useful combat, utility, and score-boosting Super Souls for TP Medals.
- Check every rotation — some strong souls only appear periodically.

### 2. Parallel Quests
- Many Super Souls are listed as possible rewards on specific PQs.
- Ultimate Finish significantly improves the chance of rare drops.
- High-star and DLC PQs (including Future Saga) are the best sources for exclusive or powerful souls.
- Some souls drop from specific enemies within a PQ rather than as a completion reward.

### 3. Expert Missions
- Strong source of both TP Medals (to buy souls) and direct Super Soul drops.
- Recommended for farming: Expert Missions 16, 17, and 18 (fast and high reward).
- Best run during double TP Medal campaigns.

### 4. Crystal Raids / Double Crystal Raids
- Broad pool of Super Souls.
- Good for filling gaps in your collection while farming other materials.

### 5. Story / Milestone / NPC Rewards
- Certain Super Souls are given for free after completing the main story or specific requirements.
- Example: **40 Ton Weights** — obtained by speaking to the NPC Nit (on top of the building near the Parallel Quest area) after finishing the main story.

### 6. Raids & Events
- Supreme Kai of Time Raid and other limited raids can drop rare high-value souls (including powerful score multipliers).
- Festival of Universes and other events periodically offer exclusive or rotating Super Souls.

### 7. Item Shop
- Basic Super Souls available for Zeni early on.

## Key Score / EXP Boosting Super Souls (High Priority for Leveling)
These are especially valuable when grinding to higher level caps (up to 199):

- **40 Ton Weights** — Doubles score earned. Easy to obtain after main story (NPC Nit).
- **My Hobbies Are Reading and Sports** — Triples score earned (sets Zeni earned to zero). Highly sought after; often tied to Supreme Kai of Time Raid or usable via customized partners.
- **Now you're upping the ante** — Random multiplier (can be x0 / x3 / x5 / x7). Available via Festival of Universes or TP Medal Shop rotations.
- **You're Not Taking This Away From Us** — Modest score + Zeni boost and improved item drop rate. Appears in TP Medal Shop rotations.

These can be stacked with in-game EXP or score campaigns for very high multipliers.

## Practical Farming Strategy
1. Unlock **40 Ton Weights** as early as possible after the main story.
2. Farm Expert Missions 16–18 for TP Medals and potential soul drops (especially during medal campaigns).
3. Check the TP Medal Shop every rotation and buy useful combat and score souls.
4. Run high-value Parallel Quests (and DLC PQs) with Ultimate Finish for exclusive souls.
5. Participate in available Raids and Festivals for rare drops.
6. Use Crystal Raids to fill remaining gaps while gathering other resources.
