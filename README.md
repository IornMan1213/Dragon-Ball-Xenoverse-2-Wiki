# Dragon Ball Xenoverse 2 Comprehensive Wiki & Resource Hub

**Last major update compiled: September 2026** (covers up to Future Saga Chapter 4 / final DLC)

This is an open community-oriented knowledge base for *Dragon Ball Xenoverse 2*. It aggregates publicly available information on unlocks, guides, builds, known issues, and tutorials. It is **not** an official Bandai Namco wiki and is not exhaustive of every single data point (the game has 100+ Parallel Quests, hundreds of skills across base +  years of DLC, and frequent free updates).

**Primary existing high-quality resources (start here):**
- [dbxv2.fandom.com](https://dbxv2.fandom.com) — Largest traditional wiki (characters, skills, mentors, quests)
- [dragonballxenoverse2.wiki](https://dragonballxenoverse2.wiki/) — Modern guides, builds, tier lists, calculators (updated 2026)
- Reddit: r/dbxv and r/DragonBallXenoverse2
- YouTube creators: Burcol (unlock guides), Steedoj, ContonMods community
